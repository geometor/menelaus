src.geometor.menelaus.duality_ceva¶

Exact projective Menelaus–Ceva point-line duality and trilinear polar constructions.

Connects geometor/menelaus directly to geometor/ceva, geometor/altshiller-court (Chapters VI & X), geometor/pappus, and geometor/etc:

  1. Projective Polarity in \(\mathbb{P}^2\) (verify_projective_menelaus_ceva_duality()): Demonstrates how standard homogeneous polarity \([x : y : z] \leftrightarrow \{xX + yY + zZ = 0\}\) maps a collinear Menelaus transversal (\(\det(D, E, F) = 0\), product \(-1\)) to a concurrent Ceva triple (\(\det(d^*, e^*, f^*) = 0\), product \(+1\)) on the dual triangle.

  2. Harmonic Conjugation & Trilinear Polars (construct_trilinear_polar(), verify_universal_trilinear_polar()): Proves that replacing each Ceva trace \((D_c, E_c, F_c)\) of a point \(P\) by its harmonic conjugate \((D_m, E_m, F_m)\) with respect to the vertices of \(\triangle ABC\) negates all three side ratios:

    \[\left(-\frac{\overline{AF_c}}{\overline{F_c B}}\right) \left(-\frac{\overline{BD_c}}{\overline{D_c C}}\right) \left(-\frac{\overline{CE_c}}{\overline{E_c A}}\right) = (-1)^3 (+1) = -1,\]

    forcing \(D_m = BC \cap E_c F_c\), \(E_m = CA \cap F_c D_c\), and \(F_m = AB \cap D_c E_c\) onto a single line: the trilinear polar \(\ell_P\) of \(P\).

  3. ETC Triangle Center Catalogue (etc_trilinear_polar_catalogue()): Evaluates exact rational trilinear polars for X(1) (anti-orthic axis), X(4) (orthic axis, planar limit of Menelaus Sphaerica III.10), X(7) (Gergonne line), and X(2) (ideal line at infinity via parallel medial sides).

src.geometor.menelaus.duality_ceva.harmonic_conjugate_collinear(P: sympy.geometry.Point, Q: sympy.geometry.Point, R: sympy.geometry.Point) → sympy.geometry.Point¶

Return the exact projective harmonic conjugate R' of R with respect to (P, Q).

If R divides PQ in signed ratio \(\lambda = \overline{PR}/\overline{RQ}\) (\(\lambda \neq 1\)), then R' divides PQ in signed ratio \(-\lambda\), satisfying \((P, Q; R, R') = -1\).

src.geometor.menelaus.duality_ceva.verify_projective_menelaus_ceva_duality() → dict[str, Any]¶

Prove exact \(\mathbb{P}^2\) polarity pairing Menelaus transversals with Ceva perspectors.

Works in homogeneous coordinates relative to the canonical projective reference triangle \(A = [1:0:0], B = [0:1:0], C = [0:0:1]\):

  • Any transversal line \(\ell = [p : q : r]^T\) (\(pqr \neq 0\)) meets \(BC (x=0)\) at \(D = [0 : r : -q]\), \(CA (y=0)\) at \(E = [-r : 0 : p]\), and \(AB (z=0)\) at \(F = [q : -p : 0]\), with \(\det(D, E, F) = 0\) and directed barycentric Menelaus product \((-p/q)(-q/r)(-r/p) = -1\).

  • Under point-line polarity \(\ell \mapsto L^* = [p^{-1} : q^{-1} : r^{-1}]\) (or isotopic reciprocal / dual basis), the dual lines through the dual vertices concur at \([p : q : r]\), with Ceva trace ratios \((+q/p)(+r/q)(+p/r) = +1\), while the trilinear polar of \(P = [u : v : w]\) is the exact dual line \(\frac{x}{u} + \frac{y}{v} + \frac{z}{w} = 0\)!

src.geometor.menelaus.duality_ceva.etc_trilinear_polar_catalogue() → dict[str, dict[str, Any]]¶

Construct exact rational Ceva–Menelaus trilinear polar pairings for classic ETC centers.

Evaluated on the scalene Heronian reference triangle A=(-9,12), B=(0,0), C=(7,0) (side lengths a=7, b=20, c=15):

  • X(1) Incenter (1, 2) \(\longleftrightarrow\) Anti-orthic axis (external bisector traces).

  • X(4) Orthocenter (-9, -27/4) \(\longleftrightarrow\) Orthic axis (planar limit of Menelaus Sphaerica III.10).

  • X(7) Gergonne point (-27/19, 24/19) \(\longleftrightarrow\) Gergonne line (perspectivity axis of the contact triangle).

  • X(2) Centroid (-2/3, 4) \(\longleftrightarrow\) Ideal line at infinity (verified by exact parallelism of the medial triangle sides \(E_c F_c \parallel BC\), etc.).
