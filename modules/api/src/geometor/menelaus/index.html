src.geometor.menelaus¶

GEOMETOR Menelaus: Digital Scriptorium Codex of Sphaerica and Exact Symbolic Transversals.

Provides 100% exact SymPy & geometor.model constructions and theorems for:

  • Planar Menelaus & Converse (geometor.menelaus.planar_transversal): Euclidean parallel-auxiliary proof (Ptolemy Almagest I.13 / Halley Lemma I), universal polynomial converse, External Angle-Bisector Axis, Desargues’s Axis, Pascal’s Hexagram, Monge’s Three-Circle Similicenters, and Wildberger Rational Trigonometry invariants.

  • Intrinsic Spherical Geometry & Spherical Menelaus (geometor.menelaus.spherical): Exact 3D unit-sphere solver (\(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1\)), spherical excess (\(A+B+C > \pi\)), polar triangle duality & AAA congruence (Sphaerica I.17), Spherical Menelaus (Sphaerica III.1) in both Ptolemaic double-arc chords and sines, spherical cross-ratio pencil invariance (III.8), and spherical orthocenter concurrency (III.10).

  • Projective Menelaus–Ceva Duality (geometor.menelaus.duality_ceva): Homogeneous \(\mathbb{P}^2\) polarity, complete-quadrangle harmonic conjugation, and trilinear polars of classic ETC triangle centers (X(1), X(2), X(4), X(7)).
