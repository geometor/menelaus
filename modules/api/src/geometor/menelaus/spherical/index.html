src.geometor.menelaus.spherical¶

Exact 3D unit-sphere solver, intrinsic spherical invariants, and Spherical Menelaus Theorem.

Implements 100% exact symbolic spherical geometry on \(S^2 = \{(x, y, z) : x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1\}\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and closed-form quadratic radical extensions (zero float or sp.Float):

  • SphericalPoint, GreatCircle, and SphericalTriangle.

  • Book I Intrinsic Spherical Foundations:

    • Spherical triangle inequality (Sphaerica I.5, Halley p. 6).

    • Spherical angle-sum excess \(A + B + C > \pi\) (Sphaerica I.11, Halley p. 11), certified purely rationally via the Euler–Eriksson half-excess tangent \(\tan(E/2) = |\det(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B},\mathbf{C})| / (1 + \mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} + \mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{C} + \mathbf{C}\cdot\mathbf{A}) \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}\).

    • Polar triangle duality \(\triangle A^*B^*C^*\) and AAA Spherical Congruence (Sphaerica I.17, Halley pp. 18–19).

  • Book III Spherical Menelaus Theorem (*Theorema Menelai*, Prop. III.1, Halley pp. 80–82; Ptolemy *Syntaxis* I.13, Heiberg pp. 74–76):

    • Evaluated both in Ptolemaic chords of double arcs \(\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{PQ}) = 2\|\mathbf{P}\times\mathbf{Q}\|\) and directed sines \(\sin_{\pm}\widehat{PQ}\) on rational points of \(S^2(\mathbb{Q})\), together with Menelaus’s central gnomonic projection from \(O=(0,0,0)\) onto a planar transversal and a universal symbolic 3D vector proof.

  • Spherical Anharmonic / Cross-Ratio Pencil Invariance (*Sphaerica* Prop. III.8, Halley pp. 93–94) and Concurrency of Spherical Altitudes (*Sphaerica* Prop. III.10, Halley pp. 95–97).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.double_arc_chord(P: SphericalPoint, Q: SphericalPoint) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute Ptolemy’s chord of the double arc \(\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{PQ}) = 2\sin\widehat{PQ}\).

In Ptolemy’s Syntaxis Mathematica I.13 (Heiberg pp. 74–76) and Menelaus’s Greek/Hebrew tradition (Halley 1758 p. 82 Scholion: subtensa dupli arcus), spherical trigonometric ratios are stated in terms of chords subtending double the given arcs on a unit-radius sphere: \(\operatorname{crd}(2\theta) = 2\sin\theta = 2\|\mathbf{P}\times\mathbf{Q}\|\).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.signed_spherical_sine_ratio(P: SphericalPoint, Q: SphericalPoint, R: SphericalPoint) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute the exact directed spherical sine ratio \(\frac{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{PR}}{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{RQ}}\).

For R on the great circle PQ (\(\mathbf{R} = \alpha\mathbf{P} + \beta\mathbf{Q}\)):

  • \(\mathbf{P}\times\mathbf{R} = \beta(\mathbf{P}\times\mathbf{Q})\) and \(\mathbf{R}\times\mathbf{Q} = \alpha(\mathbf{P}\times\mathbf{Q})\),

  • so the directed ratio of oriented cross products along \(\mathbf{n}_{PQ} = \mathbf{P}\times\mathbf{Q}\) equals \(\beta / \alpha\), which is rational whenever P, Q and the cutting great-circle normal are rational!

  • Positive when R lies on the minor arc PQ (or opposite minor arc), negative when R divides PQ externally.

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.spherical_excess_report(tri: SphericalTriangle) → dict[str, Any]¶

Certify Menelaus Sphaerica Prop. I.11 (\(A + B + C > \pi\)) and Spherical Law of Sines (III.2).

Uses the Euler–Eriksson exact rational formula for the half-excess tangent:

\[\tan\!\left(\frac{E}{2}\right) = \frac{|\det(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C})|}{1 + \mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} + \mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{C} + \mathbf{C}\cdot\mathbf{A}},\]

where \(E = A + B + C - \pi \in (0, 2\pi)\) is the spherical excess (and area on unit \(S^2\)). Also verifies the Spherical Law of Sines (Sphaerica III.2, Halley p. 86): \(\frac{\sin A}{\sin a} = \frac{\sin B}{\sin b} = \frac{\sin C}{\sin c}\).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.polar_triangle(tri: SphericalTriangle) → tuple[SphericalTriangle, dict[str, Any]]¶

Construct the exact polar triangle \(\triangle A^*B^*C^*\) and verify AAA Congruence (Prop. I.17).

Defines \(\mathbf{A}^* = \operatorname{sgn}(\det)\frac{\mathbf{B}\times\mathbf{C}}{\|\mathbf{B}\times\mathbf{C}\|}\) (and cyclically \(\mathbf{B}^*, \mathbf{C}^*\)) so that \(\mathbf{A}^*\cdot\mathbf{A} > 0\).

Verifies the exact supplementary duality laws:

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\cos a^* = -\cos A,\quad \cos b^* = -\cos B,\quad \cos c^* = -\cos C,\\\cos A^* = -\cos a,\quad \cos B^* = -\cos b,\quad \cos C^* = -\cos c,\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

proving that fixing the three dihedral angles \((A, B, C)\) of a spherical triangle uniquely determines the three side lengths \((a^*, b^*, c^*)\) of its polar triangle, hence (by SSS congruence, Sphaerica I.8) uniquely determines \(\triangle A^*B^*C^*\) and its polar \((\triangle A^*B^*C^*)^* = \triangle ABC\) (AAA Spherical Congruence, Sphaerica I.17, Halley 1758 pp. 18–19).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.verify_spherical_menelaus(tri: SphericalTriangle, transversal: GreatCircle) → SphericalMenelausResult¶

Intersect tri’s extended great-circle sides with transversal and prove Sphaerica III.1.

Verifies to exact 0 in SymPy:

  1. Signed Spherical Sine Law: \(\frac{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{AF}}{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{FB}} \cdot \frac{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{BD}}{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{DC}} \cdot \frac{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{CE}}{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{EA}} = -1\).

  2. Ptolemy / Halley Double-Arc Chord Compound Ratio (Almagest I.13, Heiberg pp. 74–76; Halley 1758 Prop. III.1, pp. 80–82): \(\frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{AF})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{FB})} = \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{AE})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{EC})} \cdot \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{DC})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{BD})}\) (equivalently, unsigned product \(= +1\)).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.spherical_menelaus_default() → SphericalMenelausResult¶

Construct the canonical rational \(S^2(\mathbb{Q})\) Spherical Menelaus configuration.

Uses exact rational vertices:

  • \(A = (0, 0, 1)\) (North Pole),

  • \(B = (3/5, 4/5, 0)\) (Pythagorean point on the equator),

  • \(C = (2/3, 2/3, 1/3)\) (rational point on \(S^2(\mathbb{Q})\)),

cut by the transversal great circle with rational normal \(\mathbf{n}_t = (2, -3, 1)\) (so \(F\) divides \(AB\) internally, \(E\) divides \(CA\) internally, and \(D\) divides \(BC\) externally, directly mirroring Halley 1758 Prop. III.1’s diagram!).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.gnomonic_projection_menelaus(res: SphericalMenelausResult) → dict[str, Any]¶

Reproduce Menelaus’s & Ptolemy’s 3D-to-2D central projection proof (Sphaerica III.1, Halley p. 80).

Projects the six unit vectors A, B, C, D, E, F from sphere center O=(0,0,0) onto the affine plane \(\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{x} = 1\) (with \(\mathbf{u} = (1, 1, 1)\)), obtaining planar points \(A', B', C', D', E', F'\) where great circles become straight lines! Verifies that Planar Menelaus holds on \(\triangle A'B'C'\) and that multiplying by the telescoping radial factors \(\frac{\|OA\|}{\|OB\|}\cdot\frac{\|OB\|}{\|OC\|}\cdot\frac{\|OC\|}{\|OA\|} = 1\) yields Spherical Menelaus.

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.verify_spherical_menelaus_universal() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Prove Spherical Menelaus universally over symbolic 3D vectors A, B, C, nt.

For any non-coplanar vectors \(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C}\) and transversal plane normal \(\mathbf{n}_t\), the ray intersections \(\mathbf{v}_D = (\mathbf{B}\times\mathbf{C})\times\mathbf{n}_t\), \(\mathbf{v}_E = (\mathbf{C}\times\mathbf{A})\times\mathbf{n}_t\), and \(\mathbf{v}_F = (\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B})\times\mathbf{n}_t\) satisfy:

\[\frac{(\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{v}_F)\cdot(\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B})}{(\mathbf{v}_F\times\mathbf{B})\cdot(\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B})} = -\frac{\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{n}_t}{\mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{n}_t},\]

whose cyclic product around \(\triangle ABC\) simplifies identically to \(-1\).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.spherical_cross_ratio(P1: SphericalPoint, P2: SphericalPoint, P3: SphericalPoint, P4: SphericalPoint) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute the exact spherical sine cross-ratio \((P_1, P_2; P_3, P_4)_{S^2}\) along a great circle:

\[(P_1, P_2; P_3, P_4)_{S^2} = \frac{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_1 P_3}\cdot\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_2 P_4}}{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_2 P_3}\cdot\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_1 P_4}}.\]

Note: Using signed_spherical_sine_ratio(), notice that \(\frac{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_1 P_3}}{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_3 P_2}} \div \frac{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_1 P_4}}{\sin_{\pm}\widehat{P_4 P_2}} = (P_1, P_2; P_3, P_4)_{S^2}\)!

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.verify_spherical_cross_ratio_invariance() → dict[str, Any]¶

Prove Spherical Anharmonic Pencil Invariance (Menelai Sphaericorum Prop. III.8, Halley pp. 93–94).

Constructs a pencil of four great circles concurrent at apex \(V = (0, 0, 1)\) with rational tangent directions, cuts them with two completely independent oblique great circles \(g_1\) (normal \((1, 2, -3)\)) and \(g_2\) (normal \((-2, 1, -4)\)), and verifies that their spherical sine cross-ratios agree to exact 0 in SymPy:

\[(X_1, X_2; X_3, X_4)_{S^2} = (Y_1, Y_2; Y_3, Y_4)_{S^2},\]

plus verifies a harmonic pencil (\(\text{cross-ratio} = -1\), matching internal/external spherical angle bisectors in Sphaerica III.6 & III.8!).

src.geometor.menelaus.spherical.spherical_orthocenter_menelaus(tri: SphericalTriangle | None = None) → dict[str, Any]¶

Verify Menelaus Sphaerica Prop. III.10 (Halley pp. 95–97): concurrency of spherical altitudes.

Through each vertex \(A, B, C\), constructs the great circle perpendicular to the opposite side great circle \(BC, CA, AB\) (passing through its pole \(A^*, B^*, C^*\)), verifies that all three spherical altitudes concur at the spherical orthocenter \(H_{S^2}\), and verifies that the spherical Ceva product of the altitude feet \(H_a, H_b, H_c\) equals \(+1\) while their spherical harmonic conjugates lie on the spherical orthic axis (product \(-1\)).
