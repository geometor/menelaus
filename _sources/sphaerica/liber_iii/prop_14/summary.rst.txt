Analytical Summary: Oblique Ascensions Across Latitudes
=======================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.14 (Halley 1758, pp. 104–107)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0120
   :page: 104

1. **Generalization Beyond Right Triangles**:
   While Prop. III.12 compares right ascensions against ecliptic longitudes and Prop. III.13 compares oblique ascensions against right ascensions, Proposition III.14 generalizes directly to **two oblique horizons** making angles :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}` and :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Delta}` with the equator :math:`\mathrm{AB}`:

   .. math::

      \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{AH}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{H\Theta}}} \;>\; \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta K}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{K\Lambda}}}

   whenever :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}} > \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}} \ge \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma B}}` (equivalently :math:`\angle \mathrm{A} < \angle \mathrm{\Delta}`).

2. **Halley's Infinitesimal Remark (p. 107)**:
   Halley supplements the elliptical transmitted proof with a fluxional/infinitesimal derivative calculation using *"momentary arcs"* (*arcus momentanei*) :math:`d\lambda = \mathrm{\Gamma E}`, computing the rate of change of oblique ascension directly via his Scholion to II.9 (p. 70).
