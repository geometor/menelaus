Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Astronomical Horizons & Fluxional Commentary

1. Geographical Latitude Interpretation
---------------------------------------

As :person:`Axel Anthon Björnbo <bjornbo>` explains (:cite:`1902, pp. 109–111 <bjornbo-1902>`, folio ``bjornbo_1902_studien_menelaos:0123``), Proposition III.14 compares rising-time ratios for two terrestrial latitudes (with angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}` closer to the pole and :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Delta}` closer to the equator), proving that extreme inequality in oblique ascensions grows monotonically as terrestrial latitude increases toward the arctic circle.
