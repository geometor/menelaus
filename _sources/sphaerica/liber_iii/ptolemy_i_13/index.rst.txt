Preliminaries to Spherical Proofs (pp. 68–75)
---------------------------------------------

**[§3.5 · Original Translation from Heiberg's Greek Text, pp. 68–69, 74–75]**

The Planar Transversal Lemma (Heiberg pp. 68–69)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. epigraph::

   *"Across two straight lines* :math:`\mathrm{AB}` *and* :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}`, *let two straight lines* :math:`\mathrm{BE}` *and* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *be drawn cutting one another at point* :math:`\mathrm{Z}`. *I say that the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma A}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{AE}` *is compounded of the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta Z}` *and the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{ZB}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{BE}`.

   *For let* :math:`\mathrm{EH}` *be drawn through* :math:`\mathrm{E}` *parallel to* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}`. *Since* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *and* :math:`\mathrm{EH}` *are parallel, the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma A}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{EA}` *is the same as that of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{EH}` [Euclid *Elements* VI.4]. *And* :math:`\mathrm{Z\Delta}` *being intermediate: the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{EH}` *shall be compounded of the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta Z}` *and that of* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta Z}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{HE}`... *And furthermore the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta Z}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{HE}` *is the same as that of* :math:`\mathrm{ZB}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{BE}` [Euclid VI.4] *because* :math:`\mathrm{EH}` *and* :math:`\mathrm{Z\Delta}` *are again parallel. Therefore the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma A}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{AE}` *is compounded of the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta Z}` *and the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{ZB}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{BE}`: *which it was required to demonstrate.*

   *And in the very same way it shall be proved* :term:`by division <compositio-et-divisio-rationis>` (*κατὰ διαίρεσιν*) *that the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma E}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{EA}` *is compounded of the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma Z}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta Z}` *and the ratio of* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta B}` *to* :math:`\mathrm{BA}`."

The Spherical Theorem (Heiberg pp. 74–75)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. folio:: ptolemy_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0084
   :page: 74

.. picture:: 0085.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated vector diagram for folio 0085.g1

.. picture:: 0085.g1.png
   :alt: Archival scan of folio 0085.g1

.. epigraph::

   *"These lemmas having been premised, let arcs of great circles be described upon a spherical surface such that two arcs* :math:`\mathrm{BE}` *and* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` *drawn across two arcs* :math:`\mathrm{AB}` *and* :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` *cut one another at point* :math:`\mathrm{Z}`; *and let each of them be less than a semicircle: and let the same be understood in all configurations.*

   *I say then that the ratio of the* :term:`chord subtending double the arc <subtensa-dupli-arcus>` :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma E}` *to the chord subtending double the arc* :math:`\mathrm{EA}` *is compounded of the ratio of the chord of double* :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma Z}` *to the chord of double* :math:`\mathrm{Z\Delta}`, *and the ratio of the chord of double* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta B}` *to the chord of double* :math:`\mathrm{BA}`."
