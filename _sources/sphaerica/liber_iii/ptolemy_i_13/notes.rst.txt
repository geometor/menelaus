Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: The Menelaus–Ptolemy Chronology & Witness
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: *Philological Context of the Almagest I.13 Witness*

.. folio:: ptolemy_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0078
   :page: 68

1. Historical Witness and Relative Chronology
---------------------------------------------

As noted by Edmond Halley (1758, p. 82) and Axel Anthon Björnbo (1902, §2):

* Claudius Ptolemy records two astronomical observations made by **Menelaus the Geometer** in Rome in the first year of Trajan (98 CE; *Syntaxis* VII.3, Heiberg II, p. 30).
* Ptolemy completed his *Syntaxis Mathematica* around 140–150 CE, roughly forty years after Menelaus's recorded observations.
* Ptolemy adopts the transversal theorem in *Almagest* I.13 without naming Menelaus, reflecting standard ancient practice when adopting common geometric tools. However, Theon of Alexandria (4th c. CE) and Pappus of Alexandria (c. 320 CE) explicitly cite Menelaus as the author of the *Sphaerica*.

2. Heiberg's Teubner Critical Edition (1898)
--------------------------------------------

The text in `ptolemy_1898_syntaxis_vol1_pt1:0078` is based on Johan Ludvig Heiberg's critical Teubner edition, drawing on the primary Greek witnesses:
- Codex Parisinus Graecus 2389 (9th century, ms. A).
- Codex Vaticanus Graecus 1594 (9th century, ms. B).
- Codex Marcianus Graecus 313 (10th century, ms. C).
