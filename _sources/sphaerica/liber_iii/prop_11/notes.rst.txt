Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: The Book of Cyclical Lemmas & Numbering Concordance
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: *Witness Collation across Halley (1758) & Björnbo (1902)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0113
   :page: 97

1. Reference to the *Liber Lemmatum Cyclicorum*
-----------------------------------------------

At the conclusion of Proposition III.11 (p. 98, folio ``0114``), Halley's Latin text notes that the full demonstrations of the difference-ratio bounds depend upon *"the first part of the Book of Cyclical Lemmas"* (*libri Lemmatum Cyclicorum*), a lost work by Menelaus or an earlier Alexandrian source, and therefore appends the geometric proof of :math:`\alpha / \beta > \sin \alpha / \sin \beta` with three corollaries (pp. 98–99).

2. Halley vs. Arabic/Björnbo Numbering (III.11–25)
--------------------------------------------------

In Halley's Oxford edition (1758), Book III concludes with five substantial propositions (**Props. III.11–III.15**, pp. 97–112). In the Arabic recension of Abū Naṣr Manṣūr analyzed by :person:`Axel Anthon Björnbo <bjornbo>` (:cite:`1902, pp. 105–123 <bjornbo-1902>`, folios ``bjornbo_1902_studien_menelaos:0119``–``0137``), subcases and parts of Halley's III.11–III.15 are numbered individually as **Props. III.11–III.25**.
