Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Acute/Obtuse Horizon Cases & Halley's Scholion
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: *Witness Collation across Halley (1758) & Björnbo (1902)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0117
   :page: 101

1. Textual Structure (Halley pp. 101–104)
-----------------------------------------

Halley prints two horizon orientations on pp. 102–103 (corresponding to the two quadrants of the zodiac: Libra–Cancer vs. Aries–Gemini in Björnbo's diagrams), followed on pp. 103–104 by an explanatory **Scholion** reconstructing the reduction of Prop. III.13 to Prop. III.11 via an auxiliary triangle construction.

2. Witness Concordance
----------------------

* **Halley (1758)**: Book III Prop. XIII & Scholion, pp. 101–104 (folios ``0117``–``0120``).
* **Björnbo (1902)**: Prop. III, 13 (pp. 108–109, folios ``bjornbo_1902_studien_menelaos:0122``–``0123``).
