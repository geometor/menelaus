Analytical Summary: Oblique vs. Right Ascension Differences
===========================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.13 (Halley 1758, pp. 101–104)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0117
   :page: 101

1. **Geometric Reduction via Prop. III.4**:
   Given triangle :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}` with side :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}} > \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma B}}` (neither exceeding a quadrant), cevians :math:`\mathrm{\Delta E}, \mathrm{ZH}` parallel in equal base inclination (:math:`\angle \mathrm{E} = \angle \mathrm{H} = \angle \mathrm{A}`), and altitudes :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Theta}, \mathrm{\Delta K}, \mathrm{Z\Lambda}`, Prop. III.4 yields constant sine ratios along the perpendicular projections on base :math:`\mathrm{AB}`:

   .. math::

      \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{A\Theta}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Theta B}}}
      \;=\;
      \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{EK}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{KB}}}
      \;=\;
      \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{H\Lambda}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{B\Lambda}}}.

2. **Chained Inequality Bounds**:
   Combining Prop. III.11 (via the auxiliary construction in the Scholion, pp. 103–104) with Prop. III.12 establishes the transitive chain of ratio inequalities:

   .. math::

      \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{AE}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{EH}}}
      \;>\;
      \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta K}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{K\Lambda}}}
      \;>\;
      \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta Z}}}.

   Astronomically, this bounds differences of oblique ascensions (:math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AE}}, \widehat{\mathrm{EH}}`) against right ascensions (:math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta K}}, \widehat{\mathrm{K\Lambda}}`) and ecliptic longitudes (:math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}}, \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta Z}}`) across all regions of the ecliptic.
