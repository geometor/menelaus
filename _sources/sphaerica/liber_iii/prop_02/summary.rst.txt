Analytical Summary: The Spherical Law of Sines
==============================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.2 (Halley 1758, pp. 86–87)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0102
   :page: 86

1. **Enunciation & Converse**:
   Two spherical triangles :math:`\triangle AB\Gamma` and :math:`\triangle \Delta EZ` with :math:`\angle A = \angle \Delta` and :math:`\angle \Gamma = \angle Z` (or :math:`\angle \Gamma + \angle Z = \pi`) satisfy :math:`\sin \widehat{AB} : \sin \widehat{B\Gamma} = \sin \widehat{\Delta E} : \sin \widehat{EZ}`, and conversely.

2. **Halley's Corollary — Spherical Law of Sines**:
   By intersecting the two triangles at vertex :math:`A` and applying Proposition III.1 to cancel the equal or supplementary sine factors :math:`\sin \widehat{\Gamma K} = \sin \widehat{KH}`, Proposition III.2 immediately implies the universal **Spherical Law of Sines**:

   .. math::

      \frac{\sin a}{\sin A} \;=\; \frac{\sin b}{\sin B} \;=\; \frac{\sin c}{\sin C}.
