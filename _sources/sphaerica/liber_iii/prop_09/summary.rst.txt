Analytical Summary: Incenter Concurrency & Incircle Existence on S²
===================================================================

.. rubric:: *Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Proposition III.9*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0111
   :page: 95

1. Synthetic Analysis of Angle Bisector Concurrency
---------------------------------------------------

* **Given**: Spherical triangle :math:`\triangle AB\Gamma` with bisectors :math:`A\Delta` and :math:`\Gamma\Delta` meeting at :math:`\Delta`. Arc :math:`B\Delta E` connects vertex :math:`B` to opposite side :math:`A\Gamma`.
* **Demonstrate**: Arc :math:`B\Delta E` bisects angle :math:`\angle B`.
* **Proof**:
  - Prop. III.6 on :math:`\triangle ABE` with bisector :math:`A\Delta`: :math:`\sin \widehat{B\Delta} : \sin \widehat{\Delta E} = \sin \widehat{BA} : \sin \widehat{AE}`.
  - Prop. III.6 on :math:`\triangle \Gamma BE` with bisector :math:`\Gamma\Delta`: :math:`\sin \widehat{B\Delta} : \sin \widehat{\Delta E} = \sin \widehat{B\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{\Gamma E}`.
  - Equating the common ratio: :math:`\sin \widehat{BA} : \sin \widehat{AE} = \sin \widehat{B\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{\Gamma E}`.
  - Alternating (*permutando*): :math:`\sin \widehat{B\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{BA} = \sin \widehat{\Gamma E} : \sin \widehat{AE}`.
  - Converse of Prop. III.6 forces :math:`B\Delta` to bisect :math:`\angle B`.

2. The Spherical Incircle Corollary
-----------------------------------

* The common intersection :math:`\Delta` is equidistant from all three sides in spherical perpendicular arc measure.
* Therefore, a spherical small circle with center :math:`\Delta` and radius :math:`r` touches all three great-circle sides, establishing the existence of the **inscribed circle** for every spherical triangle.
