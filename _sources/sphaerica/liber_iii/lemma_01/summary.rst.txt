Analytical Summary: Planar Menelaus Transversal Theorem
=======================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Book III Lemma I (Halley 1758, p. 83)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0099
   :page: 83

1. **Deductive Function**:
   Lemma I establishes the Planar Menelaus Transversal Theorem for a complete quadrilateral in the Euclidean plane. By drawing a single auxiliary parallel line through :math:`A` (:math:`AH \parallel BE`) for division (*katà diaíresin*), or through :math:`E` (:math:`E\Theta \parallel \Gamma\Delta`) for composition (*katà sýnthesin*), Euclid *Elements* VI.4 similar triangles immediately compound the two segment ratios across an intermediate mean term :math:`Z\Delta`.

2. **Role in Proposition III.1**:
   When the secant plane of a great-circle transversal intersects the 3D chordal triangle formed by three points on :math:`S^2`, Lemma I governs the resulting collinear points in the secant plane, before Lemma II translates each chord ratio into a spherical sine ratio.
