Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Apollonius's Lost Treatise & Arabic III.21–25
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: *Witness Collation across Halley (1758) & Björnbo (1902)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0123
   :page: 107

1. Reference to Apollonius (*Liber הכללי*) & Theodosius III.11
---------------------------------------------------------------

On page 108 (folio ``0124``), Menelaus cites a lost treatise by Apollonius titled *Liber* ``הכללי`` (*De Principiis universalibus*) in connection with proving the principal proposition of Book III of :person:`Theodosius <theodosius>`'s *Sphaerica* (III.11) universally. Both the first part (mean proportional between the sphere diameter and tropic circle diameter) and the sharp two-sided differential bounds anticipate early calculus-like extremum arguments (:math:`\frac{d\alpha}{d\lambda} \propto \frac{1}{\cos^2 \delta}`).

2. Halley III.15 vs. Arabic/Björnbo Props. III.15–25
----------------------------------------------------

In Halley's Latin edition (following Gerard of Cremona / the Hebrew witness), Proposition III.15 unites four major sections and multiple subcases spanning pages 107–112 and concluding with ``FINIS.`` As :person:`Axel Anthon Björnbo <bjornbo>` explains (:cite:`1902, pp. 114–123 <bjornbo-1902>`, folios ``bjornbo_1902_studien_menelaos:0128``–``0137``), the Arabic redaction of Abū Naṣr Manṣūr divides these four parts (together with the intermediate lemmas) into distinct numbered propositions **III.15–III.25**.
