Analytical Summary: Rule of Four Quantities & Spherical Tangents
================================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.3 (Halley 1758, pp. 87–88)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0103
   :page: 87

1. **Right-Triangle Proportion**:
   In two right-angled spherical triangles :math:`\triangle AB\Gamma` (right at :math:`A`) and :math:`\triangle \Delta EZ` (right at :math:`\Delta`) with equal acute angle :math:`\angle \Gamma = \angle Z`, using the polar complements :math:`\widehat{BH} = 90^\circ - \widehat{AB}` and :math:`\widehat{\Theta E} = 90^\circ - \widehat{\Delta E}` gives:

   .. math::

      \frac{\sin \widehat{AB}}{\cos \widehat{AB} \cdot \sin \widehat{A\Gamma}}
      \;=\;
      \frac{\sin \widehat{\Delta E}}{\cos \widehat{\Delta E} \cdot \sin \widehat{\Delta Z}}.

2. **Halley's Tangent Corollary**:
   Since :math:`\sin \widehat{AB} / \sin \widehat{BH} = \sin \widehat{AB} / \cos \widehat{AB} = \tan \widehat{AB}`, Proposition III.3 directly yields the **Rule of Tangents** (:math:`\tan \widehat{AB} : \tan \widehat{\Delta E} = \sin \widehat{A\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{\Delta Z}`).
