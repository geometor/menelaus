1. Deductive Architecture of Menelaus's Masterpiece
---------------------------------------------------

Proposition III.10 is widely regarded as one of the supreme technical summits of Hellenistic geometry:

1. **The Orthic Axis / Trilinear Polar**:
   Menelaus constructs the great-circle arc :math:`E\Delta\Theta` through the altitude feet :math:`\Delta` and :math:`E`, meeting base :math:`A\Gamma` produced at :math:`\Theta`. In projective geometry, :math:`E\Delta\Theta` is the **trilinear polar** of the altitude concurrence :math:`Z`.
2. **Triple Application of Proposition III.1**:
   Applying the spherical Menelaus theorem to three distinct configurations (:math:`A\Theta EZ`, :math:`A\Gamma BZ`, and :math:`AB\Gamma Z`) cancels intermediate terms, proving that :math:`\Theta` and :math:`H` divide :math:`A\Gamma` **harmonically in sines**:

   .. math::

      \frac{\sin\widehat{A\Theta}}{\sin\widehat{\Theta\Gamma}}
      \;=\;
      \frac{\sin\widehat{AH}}{\sin\widehat{H\Gamma}}.

3. **Incenter of the Orthic Triangle**:
   Using the second converse of Prop. III.8, Menelaus proves that the altitudes :math:`\Delta Z` and :math:`EZ` bisect the internal angles of the orthic triangle :math:`\triangle \Delta EH`. By Prop. III.9, the third ray :math:`HZ` must bisect :math:`\angle EH\Delta`.
4. **Deduction of the Third Altitude**:
   Applying Prop. III.6 and the first converse of Prop. III.8, the harmonic division forces the third angle :math:`\angle AHB` to be a **right angle**, proving :math:`BH \perp A\Gamma`!
