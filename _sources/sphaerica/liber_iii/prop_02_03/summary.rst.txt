Analytical Summary: Derivation of the Law of Sines & Tangent Rules
==================================================================

.. rubric:: *Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Propositions III.2 & III.3*

1. Proposition III.2: The Spherical Law of Sines
------------------------------------------------

* **Hypothesis**: Two spherical triangles :math:`\triangle AB\Gamma` and :math:`\triangle \Delta EZ` have :math:`\angle A = \angle \Delta` and :math:`\angle \Gamma = \angle Z` (or :math:`\angle \Gamma + \angle Z = 180^\circ`).
* **Conclusion**: :math:`\sin \widehat{AB} : \sin \widehat{B\Gamma} = \sin \widehat{\Delta E} : \sin \widehat{EZ}`.
* **Corollaries**: Setting :math:`\triangle \Delta EZ` to be a sub-triangle formed by an altitude dropped from any vertex of an arbitrary spherical triangle gives:

  .. math::

     \frac{\sin a}{\sin A} \;=\; \frac{\sin b}{\sin B} \;=\; \frac{\sin c}{\sin C}.

2. Proposition III.3: The Rule of Four Quantities and Tangents
--------------------------------------------------------------

* In two right-angled spherical triangles with equal acute angle :math:`\angle \Gamma = \angle Z`:

  .. math::

     \frac{\tan a_1}{\tan a_2} \;=\; \frac{\sin b_1}{\sin b_2}.

* This identity enabled ancient astronomers to solve right spherical triangles using four terms rather than requiring the complete six-term transversal product.
