Menelaus of Alexandria
======================

**Role**: *Alexandrian Geometer & Astronomer; Author of Sphaerica*

Greco-Roman mathematician born in Alexandria (*Μενέλαος ὁ Ἀλεξανδρεύς*, as :person:`Pappus of Alexandria <pappus>` styles him in *Synagoge* VI) and active at Rome, where :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <ptolemy>` records two precision stellar occultation observations made by Menelaus in 98 CE (*Almagest* VII.3):

* On **15–16 Tybi** (January 98 CE), Menelaus observed the star **Spica** (*α Virginis*) occulted by the Moon and emerging along the Moon's western limb.
* On **18–19 Tybi**, Menelaus measured the alignment of the northernmost star in the brow of **Scorpius** (*β Scorpii*) with the Moon's southern horn.

Plutarch (*De facie in orbe lunae*, 930A) introduces the mathematician Menelaus as an interlocutor in a philosophical dialogue at Rome. In his foundational three-book *Sphaerica*, Menelaus founded intrinsic spherical geometry—isolating the spherical triangle (*triangulum sphaericum* / *τρίπλευρον*), proving spherical angle excess and AAA congruence without Euclid's Parallel Postulate, and demonstrating the six-quantity Planar and Spherical Transversal Theorem (the *Theorem of Menelaus* or *al-Shakl al-Qaṭṭāʿ* / *Figura Catha*). Ancient sources also record his six books *On Chords in a Circle*, three books of *Elements of Geometry* (preserving a direct proof of Euclid I.25), and works on hydrostatics and higher curves.
