Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Book II Numbering & Stemmatics
==========================================================

.. rubric:: *Philological Notes on Book II Propositions*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0064
   :page: 48

1. Medieval Proposition Numbering
---------------------------------

As documented by :person:`Axel Anthon Björnbo <bjornbo>` (:cite:`1902, §§6–8 <bjornbo-1902>`), medieval manuscript traditions vary considerably in how they count propositions across Book II:

* **Gerard of Cremona's Toledo Latin Version**: 19 numbered propositions.
* **Abū Naṣr Manṣūr's 1007 CE Redaction**: 10 propositions.
* **Edmond Halley's 1758 Oxford Edition**: 12 propositions (combining several sub-cases).

2. Systematic Independence from 3D Chords
-----------------------------------------

Every witness, however, agrees on the structural core: Menelaus consistently avoids 3D chord intersections, relying entirely on intrinsic angle and arc comparisons on the surface of :math:`S^2`.
