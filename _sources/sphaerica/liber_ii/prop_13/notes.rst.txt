Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Closure of Book II & Manuscript Witnesses
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: *Philological Notes on Proposition II.13 (Halley 1758, pp. 78–79)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0094
   :page: 78

1. Numbering & Transition to Book III
-------------------------------------

In Halley's Oxford edition (pp. 78–79), this closing theorem of Book II is numbered **Prop. XIII** (whereas some Arabic/Björnbo summaries group Propositions II.12–13 together as the two cases of Prop. II.12). Halley prints the catchword *"MENE-"* at the foot of page 79 (folio ``0095``), leading directly into *"MENELAI ALEXANDRINI SPHAERICORUM Lib. III"* on page 80 (folio ``0096``).

2. Witness Concordance
----------------------

* **Halley (1758)**: Book II Prop. XIII, pp. 78–79 (folios ``0094``–``0095``).
* **Björnbo (1902)**: Chapter V (*Übersicht des zweiten Buches*, §8, folio ``bjornbo_1902_studien_menelaos:0065``).
* **Ptolemy Parallel**: *Almagest* Book II (Heiberg I, pp. 91–105).
