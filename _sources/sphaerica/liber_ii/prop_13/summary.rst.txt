Analytical Summary: Oblique Horizons Touching a Larger Parallel & Conclusion of Book II
=======================================================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition II.13 (Halley 1758, pp. 78–79)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0094
   :page: 78

1. **Geometric Scope**:
   Proposition II.13 completes Book II by reversing the latitude relation of Proposition II.12: the oblique horizons touch a parallel circle **larger** than the tropic :math:`A\Delta E` (lower terrestrial latitude), without requiring the inclination to lie on the same side.

2. **Reversed Parallel Inequality**:
   - Because :math:`\widehat{X\Theta} > \widehat{\Theta B}` in triangle :math:`\triangle \Theta BX`, Book II Prop. 9 gives :math:`\widehat{XP} > \widehat{\Xi T}` along the equator, while Book II Prop. 7 reverses the parallel arc separation: :math:`\widehat{\Theta\Sigma} > \widehat{O\Pi}`.
   - With this proposition and its converse, Book II finishes its synthetic foundation for rising times before Book III introduces the quantitative chord/sine metric.
