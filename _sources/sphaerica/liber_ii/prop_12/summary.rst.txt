Analytical Summary: Oblique Horizons Touching a Smaller Parallel
================================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition II.12 (Halley 1758, pp. 77–78)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0093
   :page: 77

1. **Geometric Scope**:
   Proposition II.12 generalizes Proposition II.10 from right ascensions (meridian circles through the equator's poles) to oblique horizons touching a parallel circle **smaller** than the tropic :math:`A\Delta E` (higher terrestrial latitude), inclined toward the same side of the equator.

2. **Inequality Conclusions**:
   - The arc cut off between parallel circles nearer the equator is larger than the arc nearer the tropic: :math:`\widehat{\Pi O} > \widehat{\Theta\Sigma}`.
   - The equatorial rising-time arc nearer the node :math:`B` is smaller than the arc further from the node: :math:`\widehat{\Xi T} < \widehat{XP}`.
