1. Unifying Theodosius *Sphaerica* III.7 and III.8
---------------------------------------------------

Edmond Halley notes at the end of Proposition II.12 (p. 78): *"Hae VIIma & VIIIva Libri III. Theodosii."* By invoking Book I Prop. 21 (proving :math:`\angle B\Theta X \le \pi/2`) and Book II Prop. 7, Menelaus handles oblique ascensions synthetically without any numerical chord table.
