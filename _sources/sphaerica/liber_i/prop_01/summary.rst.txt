Analytical Summary: Constructive Spherical Angle Transfer
=========================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition I.1*

1. **Enunciation & Classical Parallel**:
   Proposition I.1 provides the spherical constructive analogue of Euclid *Elements* I.23 (*At a given point on a given straight line, to construct a rectilinear angle equal to a given rectilinear angle*).

2. **The Spherical Polar Construction**:
   - In planar geometry, Euclid I.23 takes three arbitrary points on the legs of the given angle, constructs the chordal triangle, and transfers the triangle by SSS construction (Elements I.22).
   - On the sphere, Menelaus uses the vertex of the given angle as the :term:`pole <polus>` of a small circle, cuts equal arcs on the containing great circles, and transfers the circular arc opening to a small circle of equal radius described about the target vertex.
   - The dihedral angle between the great-circle planes is directly measured by the arc of the small circle (or polar great circle) intercepted between them, establishing congruence without bodily superposition.
