Propositions I.8–I.10 — SSS Congruence & Semicircle Curvature Criterion
========================================================================

.. rubric:: *Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. I Prop. VIII, IX, X (Halley 1758, pp. 8–10)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0024
   :page: 8

Proposition I.8 — Theorem: SSS Congruence & Greater Base
--------------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Enunciation (*Halley 1758, p. 8*)

   *If two spherical triangles have two sides equal to two sides respectively, each to each; and the base of the one be greater than the base of the other: the angle which the greater base subtends shall also be greater than the angle which the lesser base subtends; and conversely.*

**[Corollary]** If all three sides of one spherical triangle be equal to all three sides of another respectively, each to each, the triangles are mutually equiangular and congruent (SSS).

----

Proposition I.9 — Theorem: Greater Side Subtends Greater Angle
--------------------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Enunciation (*Halley 1758, p. 9*)

   *In every spherical triangle, the greater arc subtends the greater spherical angle.*

----

Proposition I.10 — Theorem: The Semicircle Curvature Threshold
--------------------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Enunciation (*Halley 1758, pp. 9–10*)

   *In every spherical triangle, if the two sides taken together are less than a semicircle, the exterior angle is greater than the remote interior angle; if equal to a semicircle, it is equal; but if greater than a semicircle, it is less: and conversely.*

**[Demonstration]** In spherical triangle :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}` with side :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` produced to :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}`, let :math:`\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}` be the exterior angle:

1. If :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}} + \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}} < \text{semicircle}`, Menelaus proves :math:`\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta} > \angle \mathrm{A}`.
2. If :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}} + \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}} = \text{semicircle}`, then :math:`\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta} = \angle \mathrm{A}`.
3. If :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}} + \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}} > \text{semicircle}`, then :math:`\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta} < \angle \mathrm{A}`.

And every one of these implications converts universally: if :math:`\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta} > \angle \mathrm{A}`, then :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}} + \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}} < \text{semicircle}`, and so forth.
