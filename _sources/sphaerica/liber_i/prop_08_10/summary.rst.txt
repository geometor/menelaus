Analytical Summary: SSS & the Semicircle Curvature Threshold
============================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Significance of Proposition I.10*

1. **Euclidean Exterior Angle Failure (Elements I.16)**:
   In Euclidean planar geometry, the exterior angle is unconditionally strictly greater than either remote interior angle (Elements I.16: :math:`\angle_{\text{ext}} > \angle_{\text{int}}`). On the sphere, this fundamental theorem of neutral geometry fails because two great circles intersect twice at antipodal points, forming a spherical lune whose sides are semicircles with equal antipodal angles!

2. **The Semicircle Sum Condition (Proposition I.10)**:
   Menelaus establishes the precise curvature-dependent criterion:
   - When :math:`a + b < \pi`, the Euclidean inequality holds: :math:`\angle_{\text{ext}} > \angle_{\text{int}}`.
   - When :math:`a + b = \pi`, equality holds: :math:`\angle_{\text{ext}} = \angle_{\text{int}}`.
   - When :math:`a + b > \pi`, the inequality reverses: :math:`\angle_{\text{ext}} < \angle_{\text{int}}`!

3. **Engine of Book I**:
   Proposition I.10 is the universal tool that unlocks both Spherical Angle-Sum Excess (Proposition I.11) and AAA Congruence (Proposition I.17).
