Proposition I.17 — Theorem: AAA Spherical Congruence
====================================================

.. rubric:: *Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. I Prop. XVII (Halley 1758, pp. 18–19)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0034
   :page: 18

.. admonition:: Enunciation (*Halley 1758, p. 18*)

   *If two spherical triangles have the three angles of the one equal to the three angles of the other, each to its corresponding angle: then the arcs containing those triangles are also equal to one another, each to its corresponding side.*

**[§1.5 · Menelaus's Direct Intrinsic Demonstration]** Let :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}` and :math:`\triangle \mathrm{\Delta EZ}` be two spherical triangles in which angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}` is equal to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Delta}`, angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{B}` to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{E}`, and angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Gamma}` to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{Z}`: I say that arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}`, arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}` to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{EZ}}`, and arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}}` to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta Z}}`.

Produce arc :math:`\mathrm{AB}` past :math:`\mathrm{B}` to :math:`\mathrm{I}`, and make :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{BI}}` equal to :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}`; and produce :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma B}` past :math:`\mathrm{B}` to :math:`\mathrm{\Theta}`, and make :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Theta}}` equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{EZ}}`; and draw the great-circle arc :math:`\mathrm{I\Theta}`, which produce to meet arc :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` also produced at point :math:`\mathrm{K}`.

1. Now the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta B}}, \widehat{\mathrm{BI}}` are equal to the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}, \widehat{\mathrm{EZ}}`, and angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{IB\Theta}` equals the vertical angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{AB\Gamma} = \angle \mathrm{E}`: wherefore arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{I\Theta}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta Z}}`, and angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{I}` to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Delta}` (which is equal to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}`); wherefore angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{I}` is equal to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}`. And angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Theta}` is equal to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{Z}` (which is equal to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Gamma}`); so that angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Theta}` is equal to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Gamma}`.
2. And since the exterior angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Gamma}` of triangle :math:`\triangle \mathrm{\Theta K\Gamma}` is equal to the interior and remote angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Theta}`, the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta K}}` and :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{K\Gamma}}` taken together will be equal to a semicircle (*by Proposition I.10*).
3. In like manner, since the exterior angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{I}` of triangle :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AIK}` is equal to the remote and interior angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}`, the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AK}}` and :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{KI}}` will also be equal to a semicircle (*by the same Proposition I.10*).
4. And accordingly the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{K\Theta}} + \widehat{\mathrm{K\Gamma}}` are equal to the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{IK}} + \widehat{\mathrm{KA}}`. Let the common arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{IK}}` and :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{K\Gamma}}` be subtracted from both sides, and the remaining arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta I}}` will be equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}}`!
5. But arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta I}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{Z\Delta}}`; wherefore arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{Z\Delta}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}}`. And angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}` is equal to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Delta}`, as angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Gamma}` is to angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{Z}`: wherefore (*by Proposition I.14, ASA*) arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}`, and arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}` to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{EZ}}`.

*Quod erat demonstrandum.*
