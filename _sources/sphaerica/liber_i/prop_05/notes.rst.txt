1. Geodesic Metric Space on S^2
-------------------------------

On the unit sphere :math:`S^2`, the intrinsic distance :math:`d_{S^2}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \arccos(\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y})` defines a bona fide metric space on any open hemisphere:

1. Non-negativity and identity of indiscernibles: :math:`d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) \ge 0`, with equality if and only if :math:`\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{y}`.
2. Symmetry: :math:`d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = d(\mathbf{y}, \mathbf{x})`.
3. Triangle inequality: :math:`d(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{C}) \le d(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}) + d(\mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C})`.

Proposition I.5 constitutes the constructive geometric demonstration that the minor great-circle arc is the geodesic path of minimal distance between points on :math:`S^2`.
