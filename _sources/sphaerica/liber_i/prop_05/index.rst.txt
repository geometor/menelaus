Proposition I.5 — Theorem: Spherical Triangle Inequality
========================================================

.. rubric:: *Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. I Prop. V. Theor. (Halley 1758, pp. 6–7)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0022
   :page: 6

.. picture:: 0022.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated vector diagram for folio 0022.g1

.. picture:: 0022.g1.png
   :alt: Archival scan of folio 0022.g1

.. admonition:: Enunciation (*Halley 1758, p. 6*)

   *Any two arcs of any spherical triangle taken together are greater than the remaining arc.*

Let :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}` be a spherical triangle: I say that any two arcs of the three :math:`\mathrm{AB}, \mathrm{B\Gamma}, \mathrm{\Gamma A}` comprehending it, taken together, are greater than the remaining arc.

Let :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}` be the greatest of the remaining arcs; and with pole :math:`\mathrm{B}` and radius :math:`\mathrm{BA}` describe circle :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta E}`, meeting arc :math:`\mathrm{B\Gamma}` produced at :math:`\mathrm{E}, \mathrm{\Delta}`. Now because :math:`\mathrm{B}` is the pole of circle :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta E}`, and arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}` is less than a semicircle, point :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma}` will not lie at the opposite pole of circle :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta E}`. Let that opposite pole be point :math:`\mathrm{H}`. Since therefore segment :math:`\mathrm{\Delta\Gamma H}`, cut off from point :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}`, is erected upon the diameter of circle :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta E}`, and arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta H}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{HE}}`, while arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}}` is less than :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta H}}`, the straight line drawn from :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma}` to :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}` will be shorter (*by Theodosius III.1*) than any other straight line drawn from point :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma}` to the circumference of circle :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta E}`: therefore the join :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma A}` is greater than the join :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}`; and consequently arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}}` is greater than arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}}`. But arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}`; wherefore the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{BA}}, \widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}}` exceed the two

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0023
   :page: 7

arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}, \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta\Gamma}}`, that is, :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}`: wherefore any two arcs comprehending the spherical triangle :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}` taken together are greater than the remaining arc. *Quod erat demonstrandum.*
