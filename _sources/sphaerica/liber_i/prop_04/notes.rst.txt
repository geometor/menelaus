1. Polar-Circle Proof vs. Trihedral Superposition
-------------------------------------------------

In Halley's Oxford text (pp. 5–6), Menelaus constructs :math:`I.4` purely intrinsically on :math:`S^2` using polar circles and Theodosius *Sphaerica* II.11–12, rather than appealing to central solid angles. Both the direct SAS theorem and its converse (given :math:`\Gamma A = Z\Delta`, then :math:`\angle B = \angle E`) are demonstrated in the same proposition.
