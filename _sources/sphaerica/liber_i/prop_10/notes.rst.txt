Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Neutral Geometry Boundaries
=======================================================

.. rubric:: *Hilbert's Axiom System & Spherical Lune Topology*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0025
   :page: 9

1. Overturning Neutral Geometry
-------------------------------

In David Hilbert's *Grundlagen der Geometrie* (1899), Euclid *Elements* I.16 is derived purely from Axioms of Incidence, Order, and Congruence (Axioms I–IV), without invoking Archimedes' Axiom (V.1) or the Parallel Postulate (IV).

Why does *Elements* I.16 fail on :math:`S^2`? Because Hilbert's Axioms of Order (Axioms II.1–II.4, Pasch's Axiom) require lines to be homeomorphic to :math:`\mathbb{R}` (infinite and open). Great circles on :math:`S^2` are homeomorphic to :math:`S^1` (closed and compact). Betweenness on :math:`S^1` is cyclic rather than linear, allowing two geodesics to cross each other twice at antipodal points :math:`\mathbf{A}` and :math:`\mathbf{\Delta}`.
