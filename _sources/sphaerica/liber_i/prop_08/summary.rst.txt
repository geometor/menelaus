Analytical Summary: Spherical Hinge Theorem & Direct Converse
=============================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition I.8 (Halley 1758, pp. 8–9)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0024
   :page: 8

1. **Enunciation & Scope**:
   Proposition I.8 is the spherical counterpart of Euclid's Hinge Theorem (*Elements* I.24 and I.25): in two spherical triangles with two pairs of sides equal, the greater included angle subtends the greater third side, and conversely.

2. **Direct Polar-Circle Proof**:
   Instead of using *reductio ad absurdum* for the converse (as Euclid does in *Elements* I.25), Menelaus constructs equal polar arcs :math:`AH` and :math:`\Delta\Theta` from poles :math:`B` and :math:`E`, reducing both directions directly to chord comparisons on equal perpendicular segments via Theodosius *Sphaerica* III.1.
