1. Historical Witness to Menelaus's Life & Observations
-------------------------------------------------------

In *Syntaxis Mathematica* VII.3 (:cite:`Heiberg II, 30, 34 <ptolemy-almagest-vii-3>`), :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <ptolemy>` records two lunar occultations of fixed stars observed by :person:`Menelaus <menelaus>` at Rome in the first year of the reign of Emperor Trajan:

* On the 15th/16th of the Egyptian month Mechir (*January 11, 98 CE*): occultation of the Spica Virginis (Alpha Virginis).
* On the 18th/19th of Mechir (*January 14, 98 CE*): occultation of the southern forehead star of Scorpio (Beta Scorpii).

These observations establish Menelaus's active floruit in Rome around 98 CE, approximately one generation prior to Ptolemy (c. 100–170 CE).

2. Manuscript Stemma
--------------------

* **Greek Archetype**: Lost in late antiquity.
* **Arabic Tradition**:
  - First Arabic translation: al-Ḥajjāj ibn Yūsuf ibn Maṭar (c. 800 CE, fragmentary).
  - Standard Arabic translation: :person:`Isḥāq ibn Ḥunayn <ishaq-ibn-hunayn>` (revised by :person:`Thābit ibn Qurra <thabit-ibn-qurra>`, late 9th century).
  - Arabic commentary: :person:`Abū Naṣr Manṣūr ibn ʿIrāq <abu-nasr-mansur>` (Khwarizm, 1007 CE).
* **Hebrew Tradition**:
  - :person:`Jacob ben Machir ibn Tibbon <jacob-ben-machir>` (Montpellier, 1273 CE), preserved in Oxford Bodleian MS Huntington 96.
* **Renaissance & Modern Editions**:
  - :person:`Francesco Maurolico <maurolico>` (Messina, 1558).
  - :person:`Edmond Halley <halley>` & :person:`George Costard <costard>` (Oxford, 1758).
  - :person:`Axel Anthon Björnbo <bjornbo>` (*Studien über Menelaos' Sphärik*, Leipzig, 1902).
