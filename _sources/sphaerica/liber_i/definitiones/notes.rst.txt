Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Intrinsic Sphere Representation
===========================================================

.. rubric:: *Exact Vector Representation on the Unit Sphere S^2 in geometor.model*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0017
   :page: 1

1. Great Circles as Planes Through the Origin
---------------------------------------------

In :class:`~geometor.menelaus.spherical.GreatCircle`, every great circle :math:`C` on the unit sphere :math:`S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3` is uniquely defined by its unit pole normal :math:`\mathbf{n} = (n_x, n_y, n_z) \in S^2`, satisfying the diametral plane equation:

.. math::

   \mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{x} \;=\; n_x x + n_y y + n_z z \;=\; 0.

Two great circles with normal vectors :math:`\mathbf{n}_1, \mathbf{n}_2` intersect along the antipodal line generated by :math:`\mathbf{n}_1 \times \mathbf{n}_2`. The intrinsic spherical angle :math:`\theta` between them is exactly the dihedral angle between their normal vectors:

.. math::

   \cos \theta \;=\; \mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2, \qquad \sin \theta \;=\; \|\mathbf{n}_1 \times \mathbf{n}_2\|.

2. Spherical Triangles & Geodesic Convexity
-------------------------------------------

In :class:`~geometor.menelaus.spherical.SphericalTriangle`, three vertices :math:`\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C} \in S^2` define an intrinsic spherical triangle if and only if:

1. They do not all lie on a single great circle: :math:`\det(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C}) \neq 0`.
2. All pairwise arc lengths are minor: :math:`\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} > -1`, :math:`\mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{C} > -1`, :math:`\mathbf{C}\cdot\mathbf{A} > -1`.
3. The three points lie within an open hemisphere: :math:`1 + \mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} + \mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{C} + \mathbf{C}\cdot\mathbf{A} > 0`.
