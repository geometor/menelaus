Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Avoiding Euclid I.16
================================================

.. rubric:: *Why Menelaus Postpones the Exterior Angle Theorem*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0025
   :page: 9

1. Architectural Significance
-----------------------------

In Euclid's *Elements*, Proposition I.18 relies on the Exterior Angle Theorem (I.16). On the sphere, however, the Exterior Angle Theorem holds only conditionally (depending on whether the sum of two sides is less than, equal to, or greater than a semicircle — proven next in Proposition I.10). Menelaus therefore reverses the Euclidean deductive order, proving Proposition I.9 unconditionally from Proposition I.5 and Proposition I.8 before establishing Proposition I.10!
