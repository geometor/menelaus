Analytical Summary: Greater Side Subtends Greater Angle
=======================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition I.9 (Halley 1758, p. 9)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0025
   :page: 9

1. **Enunciation & Scope**:
   Proposition I.9 proves the spherical counterpart of Euclid *Elements* I.18: in any spherical triangle, the greater side subtends the greater angle.

2. **Method via I.5 & I.8**:
   Rather than cutting off the shorter side from the vertex of the angle and appealing to the exterior angle theorem (Euclid I.16, which fails on the sphere for large arcs!), Menelaus marks :math:`\widehat{\Gamma\Delta} = \widehat{AB}` from :math:`\Gamma` along :math:`B\Gamma`, uses the triangle inequality (Prop. I.5) to show :math:`\widehat{B\Gamma} > \widehat{A\Delta}`, and invokes the Hinge converse (Prop. I.8) on :math:`\triangle \Gamma A\Delta` and :math:`\triangle A\Gamma B`.
