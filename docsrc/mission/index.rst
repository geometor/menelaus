mission
=======

``geometor.menelaus`` recovers, translates, models, and proves the foundational geometric architecture of :person:`Menelaus of Alexandria <menelaus>` (*c.* 70–140 CE), author of the three-book *Sphaerica* (*Sphaericorum Libri III*) and discoverer of the **Menelaus Transversal Theorem**—the bridge linking Greek spherical astronomy to modern synthetic projective geometry.

Our mission unites three pillars of the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium:

1. **Public-Domain Philological Fidelity**: Present scan-verified diplomatic Latin texts from :person:`Edmond Halley <halley>`'s posthumous Oxford edition (:cite:`Menelai Sphaericorum Libri III, 1758 <halley-1758>`, with preface by :person:`George Costard <costard>`) alongside the oldest extant polytonic Greek witness in :person:`Claudius Ptolemy <ptolemy>`'s *Syntaxis Mathematica* I.13 (:cite:`ed. Heiberg 1898 <ptolemy-almagest-i-13>`) and the stemmatic apparatus of :person:`Axel Anthon Björnbo <bjornbo>` (:cite:`Studien, 1902 <bjornbo-1902>`), paired with a 100% original English scholarly translation.
2. **Intrinsic Spherical Geometry Without Stereometric Detours**: Highlight Menelaus's decisive conceptual revolution over :person:`Theodosius <theodosius>`: isolating the :term:`triangulum sphaericum <triangulum-sphaericum>` bounded by minor :term:`great-circle <circulus-magnus>` arcs, demonstrating positive :term:`spherical excess <excessus-sphaericus>` (:math:`A + B + C > \pi`, Prop. I.11) and **AAA spherical congruence** (Prop. I.17) without appealing to Euclid's Parallel Postulate, and discovering spherical cross-ratio invariance (Prop. III.8) two centuries before :person:`Pappus <pappus>`.
3. **100% Exact Symbolic Verification (`SymPy` & `geometor.model`)**: Formalize every planar, rational-trigonometric, unit-sphere (:math:`x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1`), and projective :math:`\mathbb{P}^2` Ceva–Menelaus duality theorem over :math:`\mathbb{Q}` and closed-form radical extensions with zero floating-point approximations, keeping historical prose and executable Python strictly separated.
