Analytical Summary: Polar Interception Symmetry
===============================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition II.11 (Halley 1758, pp. 76–77)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0092
   :page: 76

1. **Core Principle**:
   Great circles passing through the pole of either of two intersecting great circles project congruent intervals from equal arcs symmetrically situated about their common node :math:`\Gamma`.

2. **Two Pole Cases**:
   - **Case 1**: :math:`Z` is the pole of :math:`AB\Delta E`, making right triangles at :math:`A` and :math:`E`, yielding :math:`\widehat{\Theta H} = \widehat{K\Lambda}` by ASA congruence.
   - **Case 2**: :math:`Z` is the pole of :math:`\Lambda\Gamma H`, making right angles at :math:`H` and :math:`\Lambda`; since :math:`\widehat{AH} + \widehat{\Lambda E} < \pi`, Book I Prop. 16 yields :math:`\widehat{H\Gamma} = \widehat{\Gamma\Lambda}` and hence :math:`\widehat{\Theta H} = \widehat{K\Lambda}`.
