1. Superseding Theodosius *Sphaerica* III.13
--------------------------------------------

As Edmond Halley notes at the conclusion of Proposition II.11 (p. 77), *"Haec est 13a III. Sphaericorum Theodosii."* By grounding the proof directly in Book I congruence (including Prop. I.16 for the second pole case), Menelaus removes Theodosius's cumbersome solid-angle chord constructions.
