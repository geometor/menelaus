Analytical Summary: Universal Rising-Time Theorem & Theodosian Critique
========================================================================

.. rubric:: *Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Proposition II.10*

1. The Epistemological Break
----------------------------

In the prefatory note to Proposition II.10 (Halley p. 75), Menelaus explicitly articulates his deductive programme:

* **Critique of Theodosius**: :person:`Theodosius <theodosius>` (*Sphaerica* III.5, 6, 9) proved rising-time properties through extrinsic three-dimensional chord configurations and multiple separate cases that Menelaus characterizes as *"scarcely sufficiently firm"* (*vix satis firma videntur*).
* **Synthetic Replacement**: By framing the sphere's diurnal parallels and oblique ecliptic circles within the intrinsic geometry of spherical triangles (:math:`\triangle M\Gamma\Theta`), Menelaus proves both the direct theorems and their converses simultaneously and universally (*modo magis universali & qui assensum extorqueat*).

2. Geometric Decomposition
--------------------------

* **Given**:
  - Sphere :math:`S^2` with great circle :math:`\Theta MB` (ecliptic) touched by parallel circle :math:`A\Delta E` (tropic) at :math:`\Theta`.
  - Greatest parallel circle :math:`BT\Xi PX` (equator).
  - Two equal ecliptic arcs :math:`\widehat{\Theta K} = \widehat{\Lambda M}`.
  - Meridians through pole :math:`\Gamma`: :math:`\Gamma\Theta X`, :math:`\Gamma KP`, :math:`\Gamma\Lambda\Xi`, :math:`\Gamma MT`.
  - Parallel circles through arc endpoints: :math:`K\Sigma`, :math:`\Lambda O`, :math:`M\Pi`.
* **Demonstrate**:
  1. Polar declination difference: :math:`\widehat{\Pi O} > \widehat{\Sigma\Theta}`.
  2. Equatorial right-ascension difference: :math:`\widehat{XP} > \widehat{\Xi T}`.
* **Proof Mechanics**:
  - In spherical triangle :math:`\triangle M\Gamma\Theta`, leg :math:`\widehat{M\Gamma} > \widehat{\Gamma\Theta}`, each leg :math:`< \frac{\pi}{2}`.
  - Applying Book I Prop. 33 and Book II Prop. 7 to equal base segments :math:`\widehat{\Theta K} = \widehat{\Lambda M}` yields angle :math:`\angle \Theta\Gamma K > \angle \Lambda\Gamma M`.
  - The equatorial arc intercepted by angle :math:`\angle \Theta\Gamma K` is :math:`\widehat{XP}`, which must therefore exceed :math:`\widehat{\Xi T}`.
  - The sum of legs :math:`\widehat{M\Gamma} + \widehat{\Gamma\Theta} > \widehat{\Lambda\Gamma} + \widehat{\Gamma K}`, whence the parallel spacing :math:`\widehat{\Pi O} > \widehat{\Sigma\Theta}`.
