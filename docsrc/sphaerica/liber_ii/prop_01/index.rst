Proposition II.1 — Problem: Oblique Spherical Angle Construction
================================================================

.. rubric:: *Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. II Prop. I (Halley 1758, p. 48)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0064
   :page: 48

.. admonition:: Enunciation (*Halley 1758, p. 48*)

   *From a point taken on the side of an obtuse-angled spherical triangle opposite to the obtuse angle, to draw an arc of a great circle which, with one of the arcs containing the obtuse angle, shall comprehend an angle equal to that same obtuse angle.*

**[§2.1 · Construction & Demonstration, Halley p. 48]** Let :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}` be an obtuse-angled spherical triangle as described, such that :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}` and :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}` are each less than a semicircle, with the obtuse angle at :math:`\mathrm{A}` (:math:`\angle \mathrm{BA\Gamma}`); and let a point :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}` be taken on arc :math:`\mathrm{B\Gamma}`. It is required to draw from point :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}` to arc :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` an arc of a great circle comprehending with :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` an angle equal to the obtuse angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{BA\Gamma}`.

Let :math:`\mathrm{M}` be the :term:`pole <polus>` of arc :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}`, and through points :math:`\mathrm{M}, \mathrm{\Delta}` let the great-circle arc :math:`\mathrm{M\Delta I}` be drawn (meeting :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` orthogonally at :math:`\mathrm{I}`); produce arc :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` to :math:`\mathrm{E}` so that :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AE}}` and :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma I}}` are equal, and draw the great-circle arc :math:`\mathrm{ME}`.

Because :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma B}} + \widehat{\mathrm{BA}}` taken together are less than a semicircle, Menelaus applies Book I Proposition I.13 (and subsequent cevian separation lemmas in Props. II.2–II.9) to locate the unique transversal arc through :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}` reproducing angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{BA\Gamma}`. *Quod erat faciendum.*
