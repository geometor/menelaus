Analytical Summary: Costard's Preface & Oxford Philology
========================================================

.. rubric:: *Transmission History of Menelaus's Sphaerica from Alexandria to Oxford*

1. **The Oxford Editorial Programme (1703–1758)**:
   George Costard locates Halley's edition within Oxford's systematic revival of classical Greek mathematics under David Gregory and Edmond Halley: Euclid (*Opera*, 1703), Apollonius (*De Sectione Rationis*, 1706), Theodosius (*Sphaerica*, 1707), and Apollonius's *Conics* with Pappus's lemmas (1710).

2. **The Lost Greek Text & Arabic/Hebrew Survival**:
   The Greek original of Menelaus's *Sphaerica* perished before the Renaissance. The text survived solely through Islamic translations: the ninth-century version of :person:`Isḥāq ibn Ḥunayn <ishaq-ibn-hunayn>` and :person:`Thābit ibn Qurra <thabit-ibn-qurra>`, and the thirteenth-century Hebrew version executed by :person:`Jacob ben Machir <jacob-ben-machir>` (Prophatius Judaeus) in Montpellier (preserved in the Bodleian Library's Huntington collection).

3. **Excision of Maurolico's Renaissance Interpolations**:
   The only printed text available to Renaissance Europe had been Francesco Maurolico's 1558 Messina edition, which heavily altered Menelaus's genuine demonstrations with Maurolico's own lemmas. Halley restored Menelaus's authentic mathematical arguments by critical collation against the Hebrew and Arabic codices.
