Proposition I.9 — Theorem: Greater Side Subtends Greater Angle
==============================================================

.. rubric:: *Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. I Prop. IX. Theor. (Halley 1758, p. 9)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0025
   :page: 9

.. picture:: 0025.g1.svg
   :alt: Calibrated vector diagram for folio 0025.g1

.. picture:: 0025.g1.png
   :alt: Archival scan of folio 0025.g1

.. admonition:: Enunciation (*Halley 1758, p. 9*)

   *In every spherical triangle, the greater arc subtends the greater angle.*

Let :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}` be a spherical triangle, and let arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}` be greater than arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{BA}}`: I say that angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}` is greater than angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Gamma}`.

On arc :math:`\mathrm{B\Gamma}` let :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}}` be made equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}`, and through points :math:`\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{\Delta}` let great-circle arc :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta}` be drawn. Since therefore the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}` taken together (*by Proposition I.5*) are greater than arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Delta}}`, and arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}` is equal to arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta\Gamma}}`, arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta\Gamma}}` will be greater than arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Delta}}`. And arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta\Gamma}}` is equal to :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}`, and :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}}` is common; hence the two arcs :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta\Gamma}}, \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma A}}` are equal to the two :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{BA}}, \widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}}`, each to its corresponding arc. And base :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta\Gamma}}` is greater than base :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{A\Delta}}`: wherefore (*by Proposition I.8*) angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{BA\Gamma}` is greater than angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{A\Gamma B}`. *Quod erat demonstrandum.*
