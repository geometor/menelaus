Analytical Summary: Spherical Triangle Inequality
=================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition I.5 (Halley 1758, pp. 6–7)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0022
   :page: 6

1. **Enunciation & Scope**:
   Proposition I.5 proves the spherical triangle inequality: for any spherical triangle :math:`\triangle AB\Gamma`, any two great-circle sides taken together exceed the remaining side (:math:`\widehat{BA} + \widehat{A\Gamma} > \widehat{B\Gamma}`).

2. **Constructive Method**:
   Rather than descending to the center of the sphere via Euclid *Elements* XI.20, Menelaus describes a circle with pole :math:`B` and radius :math:`BA` meeting :math:`B\Gamma` at :math:`\Delta`, and proves chord :math:`\Gamma A > \Gamma\Delta` via Theodosius *Sphaerica* III.1, yielding :math:`\widehat{A\Gamma} > \widehat{\Gamma\Delta}` and thus :math:`\widehat{BA} + \widehat{A\Gamma} > \widehat{B\Delta} + \widehat{\Delta\Gamma} = \widehat{B\Gamma}`.
