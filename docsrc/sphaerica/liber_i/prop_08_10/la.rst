Prop. VIII, IX & X. Theor. (*Halley 1758, pp. 8–10*)
====================================================

.. rubric:: *Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. I Prop. VIII, IX, X (Halley 1758, pp. 8–10)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0024
   :page: 8

Prop. VIII. Theor. — De Congruentia SSS (*p. 8*)
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Enuntiatio (*Halley 1758, p. 8*)

   *Si duo triangula Sphaerica duo latera duobus lateribus aequalia habeant, unumquodque unicuique; fueritque basis unius major basi alterius: angulus quoque quem major basis subtendit, major erit angulo quem subtendit minor basis; & conversim.*

----

Prop. IX. Theor. — De Arcu Majori (*p. 9*)
------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Enuntiatio (*Halley 1758, p. 9*)

   *In omni triangulo Sphaerico major arcus majorem angulum subtendit.*

----

Prop. X. Theor. — De Angulo Exteriore & Limite Semicirculi (*pp. 9–10*)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Enuntiatio (*Halley 1758, pp. 9–10*)

   *In omni triangulo Sphaerico, si duo latera simul sumpta minora fuerint semicirculo, angulus exterior major erit interiore & opposito; si aequalia semicirculo, aequalis; si vero majora semicirculo, minor: & convertitur.*

Sit triangulum Sphaericum ``ΑΒΓ``, cujus latus ``ΑΓ`` producatur ad ``Δ``: dico quod si duo latera ``ΑΒ``, ``ΒΓ`` simul sumpta minora fuerint semicirculo, angulus exterior ``ΒΓΔ`` major erit interiore & opposito ``Α``; si vero aequalia semicirculo, aequalis; sin majora semicirculo, minor: & converso modo, si angulus exterior ``ΒΓΔ`` major fuerit angulo ``Α``, duo latera ``ΑΒ``, ``ΒΓ`` simul sumpta minora erunt semicirculo, &c. **Q. E. D.**
