1. Menelaus's Direct Demonstration of Euclid I.25
-------------------------------------------------

In his *Commentary on Euclid Book I* (p. 345, ed. Friedlein), :person:`Proclus <proclus>` explicitly commends Menelaus for giving a direct constructive demonstration of the converse Hinge Theorem (Euclid *Elements* I.25), avoiding Euclid's indirect *reductio ad absurdum* argument. Proposition I.8 showcases that exact constructive technique on the sphere using polar circles and Theodosius *Sphaerica* III.1.
