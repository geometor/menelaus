Analytical Summary: SAS Spherical Congruence Without Superposition
====================================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition I.4 (Halley 1758, pp. 5–6)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0021
   :page: 5

1. **Enunciation & Scope**:
   Proposition I.4 establishes both the direct **Side-Angle-Side (SAS)** congruence theorem for spherical triangles and its immediate **Side-Side-Side (SSS)** angle converse.

2. **Intrinsic Polar-Circle Method**:
   In planar geometry, Euclid *Elements* I.4 relies on bodily superposition of one triangle upon another. On the sphere, Menelaus avoids rigid-motion superposition entirely by describing equal-radius small-circle arcs :math:`AH` and :math:`\Delta\Theta` from poles :math:`B` and :math:`E`, reducing base chord equality :math:`\Gamma A = Z\Delta` directly to Theodosius *Sphaerica* II.11–12.
