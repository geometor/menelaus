Analytical Summary: Planar Transversal & Chord-Sine Projection
==============================================================

.. rubric:: *Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Lemmata I & II*

1. Deductive Function of the Lemmata
------------------------------------

The two Lemmata subjoined to Proposition III.1 constitute the bridge between Euclidean planar geometry and spherical trigonometry:

1. **Lemma I (Planar Menelaus)**:
   Solves the complete quadrilateral problem in the Euclidean plane. By drawing a single auxiliary parallel line through vertex :math:`A` (:math:`AH \parallel BE`) or vertex :math:`E` (:math:`E\Theta \parallel \Gamma\Delta`), Euclid VI.4 similar triangles immediately compound the two segment ratios across an intermediate mean term :math:`Z\Delta`.
2. **Lemma II (Chord-Sine Lemma)**:
   Translates any division of a chord inside a circle by a central ray into the exact ratio of the sines (or half-chords of double arcs) of the intercepted circumference arcs.

2. Application to the Spherical Sphere
--------------------------------------

When a great-circle transversal plane intersects the 3D chordal triangle formed by three points on :math:`S^2`, Lemma I governs the resulting collinear points in the secant plane, and Lemma II immediately translates each chord ratio into the spherical arc sine ratio, yielding Proposition III.1 directly.
