Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: The Rule of Tangents in Spherical Astronomy
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: *Astronomical Applications & Polar-Complement Formulation*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0103
   :page: 87

1. Polar-Complement Formulation of Tangent
------------------------------------------

Because ancient Greek chord geometry did not isolate the tangent function :math:`\tan \theta` as a single primitive, Menelaus expresses :math:`\tan \widehat{AB}` cleanly as the ratio :math:`\sin \widehat{AB} : \sin \widehat{BH}`, where :math:`H` is the pole of the base :math:`A\Gamma` so that :math:`\widehat{BH} = 90^\circ - \widehat{AB}` (cosine of the altitude). Edmond Halley makes the tangent identity explicit in his Corollary on page 88 (folio ``0104``).
