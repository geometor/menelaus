Analytical Summary: Exact Sine-Difference Formula & Apollonian Extremum
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.15 (Halley 1758, pp. 107–112)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0123
   :page: 107

1. **Exact Sine-to-Sine Product Ratio (Part I, p. 108)**:
   For ecliptic arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}` projecting orthogonally via declination circles from pole :math:`\mathrm{Z}` onto equatorial arc :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}}`:

   .. math::

      \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}}
      \;=\;
      \frac{R \cdot r_{\mathrm{trop}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{Z\Delta}} \cdot \sin \widehat{\mathrm{ZE}}}
      \;=\;
      \frac{R \cos\varepsilon}{\cos\delta_1 \cos\delta_2}.

2. **Maximal Equation of Time / Rising-Arc Difference (Part II, pp. 109–110)**:
   When the parallel radius :math:`r_K = \sin \widehat{\mathrm{ZK}}` is the geometric mean :math:`\sqrt{R \cdot r_{\mathrm{trop}}}`, the difference :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{BK}} - \widehat{\mathrm{BM}}` between ecliptic longitude and right ascension attains its absolute maximum across the entire quadrant :math:`[0, \pi/2]`.

3. **Two-Sided Bounds & Infinitesimal Corollary (Parts III–IV, pp. 110–112)**:
   Establishes sharp upper and lower bounds for the finite ratio :math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}} / \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}` across every regime (:math:`\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}} \gtrless \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}`), concluding the *Sphaerica* with the differential corollary:

   .. math::

      \frac{d\alpha}{d\lambda} \;\propto\; \sec^2 \delta \;=\; \frac{R^2}{\sin^2 \widehat{\mathrm{Z\Delta}}}.
