Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Chord-Sine Equivalence & Ptolemy's Almagest I.13
============================================================================

.. rubric:: *Witness Collation across Halley (1758), Ptolemy (1898) & Björnbo (1902)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0099
   :page: 83

1. Internal & External Ray Division
-----------------------------------

Halley's Lemma II covers both the internal intersection case (proved via similar triangles :math:`\triangle AEZ \sim \triangle \Gamma EH`) and the external intersection case (*"si recta è centro circuli occurrat subtensae extra circulum productae"*, p. 84, folio ``0100``), corresponding directly to the two spherical transversal cases in Proposition III.1.

2. Halley's Appended Ptolemaic Theorem (pp. 84–86)
--------------------------------------------------

Immediately following Lemma II on page 84 (folio ``0100``), Halley appends the alternative demonstration of Proposition III.1 (*"His subjungere liceat aliud Theorema, à Ptolemaeo in loco citato usurpatum, & à Theone Alexandrino in Commentariis demonstratum"*, pp. 84–86, folios ``0100``–``0102``), which operates directly with central solids and chords as preserved in :ref:`Ptolemy's Almagest I.13 <liber-iii-ptolemy-i-13>`.
