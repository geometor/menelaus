Analytical Summary: Chord-Sine Projection Lemma
===============================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Book III Lemma II (Halley 1758, pp. 83–84)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0099
   :page: 83

1. **Chord-to-Sine Bridge**:
   Lemma II translates any internal or external division of a chord :math:`A\Gamma` by a central ray :math:`\Delta B` into the exact ratio of the perpendicular sines dropped onto that ray from the arc endpoints:

   .. math::

      \frac{AE}{E\Gamma} \;=\; \frac{AZ}{\Gamma H} \;=\; \frac{\sin \widehat{AB}}{\sin \widehat{\Gamma B}}.

2. **Greek Chord vs. Halley Sine Formulation**:
   In Ptolemy's Greek witness (*Almagest* I.13, Heiberg pp. 69–71), the same similar-triangle proportion is expressed in terms of the *chord of double the arc*, :math:`\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{AB}) : \operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{\Gamma B})`.
