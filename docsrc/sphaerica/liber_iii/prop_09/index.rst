Proposition III.9 — Concurrency of Spherical Angle Bisectors
============================================================

.. rubric:: *Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. III Prop. IX. Theor. (Halley 1758, p. 95)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0111
   :page: 95

Proposition III.9 — Theorem & Corollary: Spherical Incenter (p. 95)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Enunciation & Corollary (*Halley 1758, p. 95*)

   *If any two angles of a spherical triangle be bisected by arcs drawn across the triangle, and from the point of concurrence of those two arcs a third arc be drawn to the remaining angle: that arc shall also bisect the remaining angle.*
   **Corollary**: *Therefore a circle can be inscribed in every spherical triangle.*

**[§4.3 · Demonstration, Halley p. 95]** In :math:`\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}`, let the angle bisectors :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta}` and :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}` of :math:`\angle \mathrm{A}` and :math:`\angle \mathrm{\Gamma}` meet at :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}`, and join :math:`\mathrm{B\Delta}` produced to meet base :math:`\mathrm{A\Gamma}` at :math:`\mathrm{E}`.

Applying **Proposition III.6** first to :math:`\triangle \mathrm{ABE}` (bisector :math:`\mathrm{A\Delta}`) and then to :math:`\triangle \mathrm{\Gamma BE}` (bisector :math:`\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}`) gives:

.. math::

   \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}}
   \;=\;
   \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{BA}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{AE}}}
   \;=\;
   \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma E}}}.

Permuting (*permutando*):

.. math::

   \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{AB}}}
   \;=\;
   \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma E}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{AE}}}.

By the **converse of Proposition III.6**, arc :math:`\mathrm{B\Delta E}` bisects angle :math:`\angle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}`!

*Therefore all three spherical angle bisectors concur at the incenter* :math:`\mathrm{\Delta}`, *and a small circle can be inscribed tangent to all three sides of every spherical triangle.* *Q.E.D.*
