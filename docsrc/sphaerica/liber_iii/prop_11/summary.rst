Analytical Summary: Arc-Difference Bounds & Sine-Ratio Monotonicity
===================================================================

.. rubric:: *Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.11 (Halley 1758, pp. 97–99)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0113
   :page: 97

1. **Shift from Triangle Centers to Monotonic Sine Bounds**:
   After establishing the concurrency of spherical angle bisectors (Prop. III.9) and altitudes (Prop. III.10), Proposition III.11 initiates the closing astronomical section of Book III (Halley III.11–15, corresponding to Arabic/Björnbo III.11–25).

2. **The Fundamental Arc-Sine Inequality Lemma**:
   On page 98 (folio ``0114``), Halley supplies the key lemma: for arcs :math:`\alpha > \beta` in the first quadrant,

   .. math::

      \frac{\alpha}{\beta} \;>\; \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin \beta},

   from which follow the monotonicity of :math:`\alpha / \sin \alpha` (Corollary 1) and the four-term ratio bounds (Corollaries 2–3) governing rising-time differences.
