Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Halley's Scholion on the Lost Treatise
==================================================================

.. rubric:: *Witness Collation across Halley (1758) & Björnbo (1902)*

.. folio:: halley_1758_menelai_sphaericorum:0115
   :page: 99

1. Halley's Critical Scholion (p. 101)
--------------------------------------

On page 101 (folio ``0117``), :person:`Edmond Halley <halley>` prints a sharp editorial scholion acknowledging that the *Liber Propositionum Cyclicarum* (*Sefer ha-Temunot ha-Heqqeshiyot*) cited in Props. III.11 and III.12 has left *"brief and imperfect arguments"* (*curta et imperfecta*) across both Arabic and Hebrew manuscripts, and refers the reader forward to the explicit differential ratio bounds in the Corollaries to Proposition III.15 (p. 112).

2. Witness Concordance
----------------------

* **Halley (1758)**: Book III Prop. XII & Scholion, pp. 99–101 (folios ``0115``–``0117``).
* **Björnbo (1902)**: Prop. III, 12 (pp. 107–108, folio ``bjornbo_1902_studien_menelaos:0121``), identifying the cited treatise with Menelaus's lost *Six Books on Chords in a Circle* (``περὶ τῶν ἐν κύκλῳ εὐθειῶν``).
