Analytical Summary: Spherical Angle Bisectors & Harmonic Pairs
==============================================================

.. rubric:: *Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Proposition III.6*

1. Synthetic Analysis of Proposition III.6
------------------------------------------

* **Internal Bisector**:
  - In spherical triangle :math:`\triangle AB\Gamma`, arc :math:`B\Delta` bisects :math:`\angle AB\Gamma`.
  - Applying the Spherical Law of Sines (Prop. III.2) to :math:`\triangle AB\Delta` and :math:`\triangle \Gamma B\Delta` gives:

    .. math::

       \frac{\sin\widehat{AB}}{\sin\widehat{B\Gamma}}
       \;=\;
       \frac{\sin\widehat{A\Delta}}{\sin\widehat{\Delta\Gamma}}.

* **External Bisector**:
  - Arc :math:`B\delta` bisects the exterior angle adjacent to :math:`\angle AB\Gamma`.
  - The same Law of Sines application yields:

    .. math::

       \frac{\sin\widehat{AB}}{\sin\widehat{B\Gamma}}
       \;=\;
       \frac{\sin\widehat{A\delta}}{\sin\widehat{\delta\Gamma}}.

* **Harmonic Conjugation**:
  - Equating the two ratios shows that the internal and external bisector feet :math:`\Delta` and :math:`\delta` divide the base arc :math:`A\Gamma` in **harmonic division in sines**:

    .. math::

       \frac{\sin\widehat{A\Delta}}{\sin\widehat{\Delta\Gamma}}
       \;=\;
       \frac{\sin\widehat{A\delta}}{\sin\widehat{\delta\Gamma}}.

  - This harmonic relation becomes the foundation for Proposition III.8 and the orthocenter theorem (Prop. III.10).
