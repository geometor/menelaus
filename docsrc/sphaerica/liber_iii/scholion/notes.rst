Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Simon Bredon and the Bodleian Manuscripts
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: *Historical & Stemmatic Context of Halley's 1758 Scholion*

1. Simon Bredon and Merton College Oxford (c. 1350)
---------------------------------------------------

Halley specifically singles out Simon Bredon (c. 1300–1372), Fellow of Merton College, Oxford, and one of the prominent Oxford Calculators:

* Bredon authored *In figuram catha*, a comprehensive treatise on the Menelaus transversal configuration preserved in Bodleian MSS (e.g., Digby 168 and 178).
* Medieval Latin scholars referred to the theorem as *Figura Sector* or *Figura Catha* (a corruption of Arabic *al-qaṭṭāʿ*, the cutting/intersecting figure).

2. Halley's Hebrew and Arabic Sources at Oxford
--------------------------------------------------

As documented by :person:`Axel Anthon Björnbo <bjornbo>` (:cite:`1902, §§1–3 <bjornbo-1902>`):

* In 1706–1707, Edmond Halley was Savilian Professor of Geometry at Oxford, working closely with Edward Bernard's materials.
* Halley utilized **Bodleian MS Marsh 399** (Arabic text with Thābit's redaction) and **Bodleian MS Opp. Add. 4to, 51** (Hebrew version by Jacob ben Machir).
* Halley's edition was printed in 1758 by John Costard and Joseph Hunt after Halley's death (1742), representing one of the finest monuments of 18th-century mathematical philology.
