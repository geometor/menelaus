Analytical Summary: Quantitative Astronomical Metric & Closing Synthesis
========================================================================

.. rubric:: *Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Propositions III.11–III.25*

1. Transition from Triangle Centers to Quantitative Astronomy
-------------------------------------------------------------

* **Props. III.1–10**: Established the foundational trigonometric laws and the projective triangle center concurrences (incenter, orthocenter).
* **Props. III.11–25**: Apply the six-term transversal ratio to practical astronomical problems:
  - Non-linear variation of rising times along the ecliptic.
  - Rate of change of diurnal declination vs. right ascension.
  - Symmetrical horizon bounds across different geographic terrestrial latitudes.

2. Epistemological Completion of the *Sphaerica*
------------------------------------------------

Through these twenty-five propositions in Book III, Menelaus completes his comprehensive reform of ancient spherical geometry:
1. **Book I**: The intrinsic geometry of the spherical triangle (analogous to Euclid *Elements* I).
2. **Book II**: Great circles cutting parallel systems and qualitative rising-time inequalities (superseding Theodosius *Sphaerica* III).
3. **Book III**: The exact quantitative chord/sine metric (*Figura Catha*), proving the Law of Sines, harmonic cross-ratio pencils, and the orthocenter concurrency.
