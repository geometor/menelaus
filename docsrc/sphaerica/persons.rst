Index Nominum (Biographical Register)
=====================================

.. rubric:: *Geometers, Astronomers, Medieval Translators, and Critical Editors*

This prosopographical register documents **22 key figures** across the Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Latin, and modern projective history of :person:`Menelaus <menelaus>`'s *Sphaerica*. Every inline ``:person:`` reference resolves directly to its ``#person-<slug>`` anchor below.

----

Biographical Entries
--------------------

.. _person-menelaus:

Menelaus of Alexandria (c. 70 – c. 140 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Alexandrian Geometer & Astronomer; Author of Sphaerica*

Greco-Roman mathematician born in Alexandria and active at Rome (where Ptolemy records two stellar occultation observations made by Menelaus in 98 CE, *Almagest* VII.3). In his three-book *Sphaerica*, Menelaus founded intrinsic spherical geometry and proved the six-quantity Spherical Transversal Theorem (Book III.1).

.. _person-ptolemy:

Claudius Ptolemy (c. 100 – c. 170 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Alexandrian Mathematician & Astronomer*

Author of the *Syntaxis Mathematica* (*Almagest*), whose Book I.13 (ed. Heiberg 1898, pp. 68–76) preserves the oldest extant Greek proof of the Planar and Spherical Menelaus Lemmas in chords of double arcs.

.. _person-euclid:

Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BCE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Alexandrian Geometer*

Author of the *Elements* and *Phaenomena*. Book I of Menelaus's *Sphaerica* systematically reconstructs Euclid Book I intrinsically on the sphere without the Parallel Postulate.

.. _person-theodosius:

Theodosius of Bithynia (c. 160 – c. 90 BCE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Greek Geometer & Astronomer*

Author of an earlier stereometric *Sphaerica* in three books superseded in power and generality by Menelaus Book II (Halley 1758, pp. 75–76).

.. _person-hipparchus:

Hipparchus of Nicaea (c. 190 – c. 120 BCE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Greek Astronomer & Pioneer of Chord Tables*

Compiled twelve books on chords in a circle, preparing the trigonometric foundation that Menelaus placed upon deductive axiomatic footing.

.. _person-pappus:

Pappus of Alexandria (c. 290 – c. 350 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Late Antique Alexandrian Geometer*

Author of the *Synagoge*, preserving Greek testimonies to Menelaus Book II in Book VI and developing planar cross-ratio invariance in Book VII.

.. _person-theon:

Theon of Alexandria (c. 335 – c. 405 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Alexandrian Mathematician & Commentator on Ptolemy*

Expanded Ptolemy's *Almagest* I.13 into a multi-case analysis of internal and external Menelaus transversals (cited by Halley 1758, pp. 84–85).

.. _person-proclus:

Proclus Diadochus (412 – 485 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Neoplatonic Philosopher & Commentator on Euclid Book I*

Preserves in *In Euclidem* (Friedlein p. 345) Menelaus's direct proof of Euclid I.25 avoiding *reductio ad absurdum*.

.. _person-ishaq-ibn-hunayn:

Isḥāq ibn Ḥunayn (c. 830 – c. 910 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Baghdad Translator of Greek Mathematical Classics into Arabic*

Translated Menelaus's *Sphaerica* into Arabic, forming the archetype behind Jacob ben Machir's Hebrew version collated at Oxford by Halley (Costard 1758, p. iv).

.. _person-thabit-ibn-qurra:

Thābit ibn Qurra (836 – 901 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Harranian Mathematician & Astronomer in Baghdad*

Author of *On the Sector Figure* (*al-Shakl al-Qaṭṭāʿ*), classifying all eighteen combinatorially valid cases of Menelaus's compound-ratio theorem.

.. _person-abu-nasr-mansur:

Abū Naṣr Manṣūr ibn ʿAlī ibn ʿIrāq (c. 960 – 1036 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Khwarazmian Geometer & Redactor of Menelaus*

Completed in 1007–1008 CE the critical Arabic redaction of Menelaus's *Sphaerica* expressing Book III in sines (*jayb*).

.. _person-gerard-of-cremona:

Gerard of Cremona (c. 1114 – 1187 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Toledan Translator of Arabic Science into Latin*

Translated *Liber Menelai de Sphaericis* and Ptolemy's *Almagest* into Latin in twelfth-century Toledo.

.. _person-jacob-ben-machir:

Jacob ben Machir ibn Tibbon (Prophatius Judaeus) (c. 1236 – c. 1304 CE)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Provençal Hebrew Translator & Astronomer*

Translated Menelaus's *Sphaerica* into Hebrew at Montpellier—the Bodleian Huntington witness collated directly by Halley.

.. _person-maurolico:

Francesco Maurolico (Franciscus Maurolycus) (1494 – 1575)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Sicilian Benedictine Mathematician & First Printed Editor of Menelaus*

Published at Messina in 1558 the *editio princeps* of Menelaus's *Sphaerica*, interpolating Renaissance corollaries collated by Costard and Björnbo.

.. _person-mersenne:

Marin Mersenne (1588 – 1648)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *French Minim Friar & Mathematical Editor*

Published an abridged synopsis of *Milei Sphaerica* inside his *Universae Geometriae Synopsis* (Paris, 1644).

.. _person-halley:

Edmond Halley (1656 – 1742)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Savilian Professor of Geometry at Oxford, Astronomer Royal & Editor of Menelaus*

Established from Bodleian Hebrew and Arabic manuscripts the faithful Oxford Latin edition *Menelai Sphaericorum Libri III* (issued posthumously in 1758).

.. _person-costard:

George Costard (1710 – 1782)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Oxford Orientalist, Historian of Astronomy & Author of the 1758 Preface*

Authored the Latin preface *Benevolo Lectori* (pp. [i]–[viii]) accompanying Halley's 1758 Oxford edition.

.. _person-ceva:

Giovanni Ceva (1647 – 1734)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Italian Geometer; Discoverer of Ceva's Concurrency Theorem*

Published *De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio* (1678), pairing Menelaus's collinearity theorem (`-1`) with its projective concurrency dual (`+1`).

.. _person-carnot:

Lazare Carnot (1753 – 1823)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *French Geometer; Pioneer of Modern Projective Transversal Theory*

Placed Menelaus's Theorem at the foundation of modern synthetic projective geometry in *Essai sur la théorie des transversales* (1806).

.. _person-poncelet:

Jean-Victor Poncelet (1788 – 1867)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *French Mathematician; Co-Founder of Projective Geometry*

Developed projective cross-ratio invariance and pole-polar duality in *Traité des propriétés projectives des figures* (1822).

.. _person-bjornbo:

Axel Anthon Björnbo (1874 – 1911)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Danish Historian of Mathematics & Philologist of Menelaus*

Author of *Studien über Menelaos' Sphärik* (Teubner, 1902), reconstructing the stemma and proposition concordance of the *Sphaerica*.

.. _person-heiberg:

Johan Ludvig Heiberg (1854 – 1928)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Role**: *Danish Classical Philologist & Critical Editor of Euclid and Ptolemy*

Edited the Teubner Greek critical edition of Ptolemy's *Syntaxis Mathematica* (1898), establishing the Greek text of *Almagest* I.13.
