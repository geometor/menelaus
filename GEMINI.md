# GEOMETOR `menelaus`

Digital Scriptorium 4-track Book Codex (`:layout: book`) and 100% exact `SymPy` / `geometor.model` symbolic package for **Menelaus of Alexandria (c. 70–140 CE): *Sphaerica* (*Sphaericorum Libri III*)** and the **Planar & Spherical Menelaus Transversal Theorem**.

## Guiding Mandates

1. **100% Exact Symbolic Precision**:
   - Zero `float` or `sp.Float` coordinates in `geometor.model.Model` or `SphericalPoint` (`TypeError` enforced at construction).
   - Planar geometry uses targeted `Model(..., auto_intersect=False)` + `@proposition` (`Given` / `Construct` / `Demonstrate`) and Rational Trigonometry invariants (`quadrance`, `line_spread`).
   - Spherical geometry operates directly on exact algebraic points of $S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ ($x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$) over $\mathbb{Q}$ and closed-form quadratic radicals.
2. **Strict Public-Domain Sources & Multimodal Scan Fidelity**:
   - Primary sources are strictly pre-1929 public-domain editions: Edmond Halley's *Menelai Sphaericorum Libri III* (Oxford, 1758, with George Costard's *Praefatio*), Claudius Ptolemy's *Syntaxis Mathematica* (*Almagest*) I.13 (ed. J. L. Heiberg, Teubner, 1898), Axel Anthon Björnbo's *Studien über Menelaos' Sphärik* (Teubner, 1902), Francesco Maurolico (1558), and Pappus *Synagoge* VI (ed. Hultsch, 1877).
   - Never cite or copy post-1930 copyrighted editions or translations (e.g., Krause 1936, Rashed & Papadopoulos 2017).
   - Zero `py_file` directives embedded inside historical Book Codex tracks (`docsrc/sphaerica/unit-*`); all SVGs are standalone `.euclid-graphic` assets in `docsrc/_static/figures/` generated by `scripts/build_figures.py`.

## Key Modules (`src/geometor/menelaus/`)

- `planar_transversal.py`: Planar Menelaus forward proof (`CH || DF`), universal polynomial converse, External Angle-Bisector Axis, Desargues's Axis, Pascal's *Hexagrammum Mysticum*, Monge's Three-Circle Exsimilicenters, and Wildberger Rational Trigonometry invariants.
- `spherical.py`: Exact 3D unit-sphere solver, rational spherical excess ($\tan(E/2) = 1/17 > 0$), polar triangle AAA congruence (*Sphaerica* I.17), Spherical Menelaus (*Sphaerica* III.1) in signed sines and Ptolemaic double-arc chords, gnomonic projection bridge, spherical 4-pencil cross-ratio invariance (III.8), and spherical orthocenter concurrency (III.10).
- `duality_ceva.py`: Homogeneous $\mathbb{P}^2$ point-line polarity (`Menelaus = -1` $\leftrightarrow$ `Ceva = +1`), complete-quadrangle harmonic conjugation, and trilinear polars of ETC centers `X(1)`, `X(2)`, `X(4)`, `X(7)`.
- `render.py`, `concordance.py`, `sphinx_ext.py`, `__main__.py`: `.euclid-graphic` SVG renderer, apparatus JSON loaders (`resources/sphaerica/`), Sphinx helper extension, and `Rich` CLI summary.
