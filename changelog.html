0.1.0 (2026-11-12)¶

  • 4-Track Synchronized Book Codex (`docsrc/sphaerica/`): - Scan-verified diplomatic Latin edition from Edmond Halley’s Menelai Sphaericorum Libri III (Oxford, 1758, with George Costard’s Praefatio) and polytonic Greek primary witness from Claudius Ptolemy’s Syntaxis Mathematica I.13 (ed. J. L. Heiberg, 1898), paired with a 100% original English translation, analytical summaries, and GEOMETOR mathematical commentary across Units I–IV. - Full scholarly apparatus (concordance.rst, bibliography.rst, persons.rst, glossary.rst and resources/sphaerica/*.json) cross-referencing Halley (1758), Maurolico (1558), Björnbo (1902), Euclid’s Elements, geometor/elements, geometor/pappus, geometor/altshiller-court, geometor/ceva, and geometor/etc.

  • Exact Symbolic Geometry Engine (`src/geometor/menelaus/`): - planar_transversal.py: geometor.model Planar Menelaus proof with Euclidean parallel auxiliary (CH || DF), universal polynomial converse, External Angle-Bisector Axis, Desargues’s Axis, Pascal’s Hexagrammum Mysticum, Monge’s Three-Circle Similicenters, and Wildberger Rational Trigonometry (quadrance, line_spread, vertex_spread, cross_ratio) formulation. - spherical.py: Exact 3D unit-sphere ($x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$) solver verifying positive spherical excess ($A + B + C > pi$, Prop. I.11), polar triangle duality and AAA spherical congruence (Prop. I.17), Spherical Menelaus’s Theorem (Prop. III.1) in both Ptolemaic double-arc chords and sines, central gnomonic projection, spherical cross-ratio pencil invariance (Prop. III.8), and spherical altitude concurrency (Prop. III.10). - duality_ceva.py: Projective $mathbb{P}^2$ point-line polarity, complete-quadrangle harmonic conjugation transforming Ceva triples ($+1$) into Menelaus triples ($-1$), and exact ETC trilinear polars (X(1), X(2), X(4), X(7)). - render.py & scripts/build_figures.py: 10 interactive dark/light-adaptive .euclid-graphic SVGs.
