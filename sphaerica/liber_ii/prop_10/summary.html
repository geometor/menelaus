Analytical Summary: Universal Rising-Time Theorem & Theodosian Critique¶

Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Proposition II.10

1. The Epistemological Break¶

In the prefatory note to Proposition II.10 (Halley p. 75), Menelaus explicitly articulates his deductive programme:

  • Critique of Theodosius: Theodosius (Sphaerica III.5, 6, 9) proved rising-time properties through extrinsic three-dimensional chord configurations and multiple separate cases that Menelaus characterizes as “scarcely sufficiently firm” (vix satis firma videntur).

  • Synthetic Replacement: By framing the sphere’s diurnal parallels and oblique ecliptic circles within the intrinsic geometry of spherical triangles (\(\triangle M\Gamma\Theta\)), Menelaus proves both the direct theorems and their converses simultaneously and universally (modo magis universali & qui assensum extorqueat).

2. Geometric Decomposition¶

  • Given: - Sphere \(S^2\) with great circle \(\Theta MB\) (ecliptic) touched by parallel circle \(A\Delta E\) (tropic) at \(\Theta\). - Greatest parallel circle \(BT\Xi PX\) (equator). - Two equal ecliptic arcs \(\widehat{\Theta K} = \widehat{\Lambda M}\). - Meridians through pole \(\Gamma\): \(\Gamma\Theta X\), \(\Gamma KP\), \(\Gamma\Lambda\Xi\), \(\Gamma MT\). - Parallel circles through arc endpoints: \(K\Sigma\), \(\Lambda O\), \(M\Pi\).

  • Demonstrate: 1. Polar declination difference: \(\widehat{\Pi O} > \widehat{\Sigma\Theta}\). 2. Equatorial right-ascension difference: \(\widehat{XP} > \widehat{\Xi T}\).

  • Proof Mechanics: - In spherical triangle \(\triangle M\Gamma\Theta\), leg \(\widehat{M\Gamma} > \widehat{\Gamma\Theta}\), each leg \(< \frac{\pi}{2}\). - Applying Book I Prop. 33 and Book II Prop. 7 to equal base segments \(\widehat{\Theta K} = \widehat{\Lambda M}\) yields angle \(\angle \Theta\Gamma K > \angle \Lambda\Gamma M\). - The equatorial arc intercepted by angle \(\angle \Theta\Gamma K\) is \(\widehat{XP}\), which must therefore exceed \(\widehat{\Xi T}\). - The sum of legs \(\widehat{M\Gamma} + \widehat{\Gamma\Theta} > \widehat{\Lambda\Gamma} + \widehat{\Gamma K}\), whence the parallel spacing \(\widehat{\Pi O} > \widehat{\Sigma\Theta}\).
