Primary Editions & Ancient Witnesses of Menelaus¶

Menelai Sphaericorum Libri III (halley-1758)¶

  • Author / Editor: Menelaus of Alexandria; Edmond Halley (ed.); George Costard (pref.)

  • Edition / Locus: Oxonii (Oxford): Sumptibus Academicis, 1758 (pp. [i]–[viii], 1–112; PD)

Praefatio ‘Benevolo Lectori’ in Menelai Sphaericorum Libri III (costard-1758)¶

  • Author / Editor: Costard, George (1710 – 1782)

  • Edition / Locus: Oxford, 1758, pp. [i]–[viii] (scans 9–16; PD)

Syntaxis Mathematica (Almagest), Book I, Ch. 13: Προλαμβανόμενα εἰς τὰς σφαιρικὰς δείξεις (ptolemy-almagest-i-13)¶

  • Author / Editor: Claudius Ptolemy; J. L. Heiberg (ed.)

  • Edition / Locus: Vol. I, Pars I, Leipzig: Teubner, 1898, pp. 68–76 (PD)

Syntaxis Mathematica (Almagest), Book VII, Ch. 3 (Menelaus’s 98 CE Observations at Rome) (ptolemy-almagest-vii-3)¶

  • Author / Editor: Claudius Ptolemy; J. L. Heiberg (ed.)

  • Edition / Locus: Vol. I, Pars II, Leipzig: Teubner, 1903, pp. 25–30 (PD)

Studien über Menelaos’ Sphärik. Beiträge zur Geschichte der Sphärik und Trigonometrie der Griechen (bjornbo-1902)¶

  • Author / Editor: Björnbo, Axel Anthon (1874 – 1911)

  • Edition / Locus: Abhandlungen zur Geschichte der mathematischen Wissenschaften, Heft XIV, Leipzig: Teubner, 1902 (PD)

Universae Geometriae Mixtaeque Mathematicae Synopsis (Milei Sphaericorum Libri III) (mersenne-1644)¶

  • Author / Editor: Mersenne, Marin (1588 – 1648)

  • Edition / Locus: Parisiis: Antonius Bertier, 1644 (PD)

Pappi Alexandrini Collectionis quae supersunt, Lib. VI & VII (pappus-hultsch-1877)¶

  • Author / Editor: Pappus of Alexandria; Friedrich Hultsch (ed.)

  • Edition / Locus: Vol. II, Berolini: Apud Weidmannos, 1877, pp. 600–604 (PD)

In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii (proclus-friedlein-1873)¶
