Analytical Summary: Oblique vs. Right Ascension Differences¶

Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.13 (Halley 1758, pp. 101–104)

  1. Geometric Reduction via Prop. III.4: Given triangle \(\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}\) with side \(\widehat{\mathrm{A\Gamma}} > \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma B}}\) (neither exceeding a quadrant), cevians \(\mathrm{\Delta E}, \mathrm{ZH}\) parallel in equal base inclination (\(\angle \mathrm{E} = \angle \mathrm{H} = \angle \mathrm{A}\)), and altitudes \(\mathrm{\Gamma\Theta}, \mathrm{\Delta K}, \mathrm{Z\Lambda}\), Prop. III.4 yields constant sine ratios along the perpendicular projections on base \(\mathrm{AB}\):

    \[\frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{A\Theta}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Theta B}}} \;=\; \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{EK}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{KB}}} \;=\; \frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{H\Lambda}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{B\Lambda}}}.\]

  2. Chained Inequality Bounds: Combining Prop. III.11 (via the auxiliary construction in the Scholion, pp. 103–104) with Prop. III.12 establishes the transitive chain of ratio inequalities:

    \[\frac{\widehat{\mathrm{AE}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{EH}}} \;>\; \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta K}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{K\Lambda}}} \;>\; \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}}}{\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta Z}}}.\]

    Astronomically, this bounds differences of oblique ascensions (\(\widehat{\mathrm{AE}}, \widehat{\mathrm{EH}}\)) against right ascensions (\(\widehat{\mathrm{\Theta K}}, \widehat{\mathrm{K\Lambda}}\)) and ecliptic longitudes (\(\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma\Delta}}, \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta Z}}\)) across all regions of the ecliptic.
