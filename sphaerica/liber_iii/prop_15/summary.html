Analytical Summary: Exact Sine-Difference Formula & Apollonian Extremum¶

Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.15 (Halley 1758, pp. 107–112)

  1. Exact Sine-to-Sine Product Ratio (Part I, p. 108): For ecliptic arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}\) projecting orthogonally via declination circles from pole \(\mathrm{Z}\) onto equatorial arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}}\):

    \[\frac{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}} \;=\; \frac{R \cdot r_{\mathrm{trop}}}{\sin \widehat{\mathrm{Z\Delta}} \cdot \sin \widehat{\mathrm{ZE}}} \;=\; \frac{R \cos\varepsilon}{\cos\delta_1 \cos\delta_2}.\]

  2. Maximal Equation of Time / Rising-Arc Difference (Part II, pp. 109–110): When the parallel radius \(r_K = \sin \widehat{\mathrm{ZK}}\) is the geometric mean \(\sqrt{R \cdot r_{\mathrm{trop}}}\), the difference \(\widehat{\mathrm{BK}} - \widehat{\mathrm{BM}}\) between ecliptic longitude and right ascension attains its absolute maximum across the entire quadrant \([0, \pi/2]\).

  3. Two-Sided Bounds & Infinitesimal Corollary (Parts III–IV, pp. 110–112): Establishes sharp upper and lower bounds for the finite ratio \(\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}} / \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}\) across every regime (\(\widehat{\mathrm{\Gamma H}} \gtrless \widehat{\mathrm{\Delta E}}\)), concluding the Sphaerica with the differential corollary:

    \[\frac{d\alpha}{d\lambda} \;\propto\; \sec^2 \delta \;=\; \frac{R^2}{\sin^2 \widehat{\mathrm{Z\Delta}}}.\]
