Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Exact 3D S² Verification & Radical Cancellation¶

Exact SymPy Verification in geometor.menelaus.spherical

1. Gnomonic Projection and Tangent Plane Mechanics¶

Menelaus’s chordal proof ([§3.1], Halley p. 80) and Ptolemy’s proof (Syntaxis I.13, Heiberg pp. 74–75) both operate by intersecting rays from the sphere center \(O = (0, 0, 0)\) with a secant or tangent plane. In modern differential geometry, this is the gnomonic projection:

\[\Pi_z(x, y, z) \;=\; \left(\frac{x}{z}, \;\frac{y}{z}\right).\]

Under gnomonic projection, every great circle on \(S^2\) maps to a straight line on the projection plane, immediately transforming spherical transversals into planar transversals.

2. Pairwise Quadratic Radical Cancellation¶

In spherical_menelaus_default() and gnomonic_projection_menelaus():

  • Canonical rational points on \(S^2(\mathbb{Q})\): \(A = (0, 0, 1)\), \(B = (3/5, 4/5, 0)\), \(C = (2/3, 2/3, 1/3)\).

  • Cutting transversal plane: \(2x - 3y + z = 0\).

  • The intersection points \(D, E, F\) involve square roots: \(D \in \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{181})\), \(E = (1/\sqrt{3}, 1/\sqrt{3}, 1/\sqrt{3})\), \(F \in \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{61})\).

  • The six individual arc sines: \(\sin\widehat{AF} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{61}}\), \(\sin\widehat{FB} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{61}}\), \(\sin\widehat{BD} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{181}}\), \(\sin\widehat{DC} = -\frac{5}{3\sqrt{181}}\), \(\sin\widehat{CE} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{9}\), \(\sin\widehat{EA} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\).

  • In the signed product, all square roots cancel identically:

    \[\left(\frac{5}{6}\right) \cdot \left(-\frac{18}{5}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right) \;=\; -1.\]
