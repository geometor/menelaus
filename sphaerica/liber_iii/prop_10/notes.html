Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Orthocenter Concurrency & Projective Duality¶

Exact Vector Proofs, Collinearities & Trilinear Polarity

1. Exact Symbolic Proof of Spherical Orthocenter Concurrency¶

In spherical_orthocenter_menelaus(), Menelaus’s three-transversal proof of Sphaerica III.10 ([§4.4], Halley pp. 95–97) is verified algebraically on \(S^2(\mathbb{Q})\) alongside the concise Lagrange vector-triple-product identity for altitude normals \(\mathbf{m}_A = (\mathbf{B} \times \mathbf{C}) \times \mathbf{A}\), \(\mathbf{m}_B = (\mathbf{C} \times \mathbf{A}) \times \mathbf{B}\), \(\mathbf{m}_C = (\mathbf{A} \times \mathbf{B}) \times \mathbf{C}\):

\[\mathbf{m}_A + \mathbf{m}_B + \mathbf{m}_C \;\equiv\; \mathbf{0} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \det(\mathbf{m}_A, \mathbf{m}_B, \mathbf{m}_C) \;=\; 0,\]

and the spherical orthocenter \(\mathbf{H} \propto \mathbf{m}_A \times \mathbf{m}_B\) satisfies exact dihedral orthogonality \((\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{H})\cdot(\mathbf{B}\times\mathbf{C}) = (\mathbf{B}\times\mathbf{H})\cdot(\mathbf{C}\times\mathbf{A}) = (\mathbf{C}\times\mathbf{H})\cdot(\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B}) = 0\) on all three sides simultaneously. Remarkably, Menelaus’s auxiliary great circle \(E\Delta\Theta\) joining altitude feet \(\Delta\) and \(E\) is literally the orthic axis—the trilinear polar of the orthocenter \(Z\)!

  • verify_projective_menelaus_ceva_duality() proves that under homogeneous point-line polarity \([x : y : z] \leftrightarrow xX + yY + zZ = 0\) in \(\mathbb{P}^2\), Menelaus’s collinearity of three side-cuts (\(-1\)) and Giovanni Ceva’s concurrency of three vertex-joins (\(+1\)) are algebraically dual theorems!
