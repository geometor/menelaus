Analytical Summary: Rule of Four Quantities & Spherical Tangents¶

Deductive Decomposition of Proposition III.3 (Halley 1758, pp. 87–88)

  1. Right-Triangle Proportion: In two right-angled spherical triangles \(\triangle AB\Gamma\) (right at \(A\)) and \(\triangle \Delta EZ\) (right at \(\Delta\)) with equal acute angle \(\angle \Gamma = \angle Z\), using the polar complements \(\widehat{BH} = 90^\circ - \widehat{AB}\) and \(\widehat{\Theta E} = 90^\circ - \widehat{\Delta E}\) gives:

    \[\frac{\sin \widehat{AB}}{\cos \widehat{AB} \cdot \sin \widehat{A\Gamma}} \;=\; \frac{\sin \widehat{\Delta E}}{\cos \widehat{\Delta E} \cdot \sin \widehat{\Delta Z}}.\]

  2. Halley’s Tangent Corollary: Since \(\sin \widehat{AB} / \sin \widehat{BH} = \sin \widehat{AB} / \cos \widehat{AB} = \tan \widehat{AB}\), Proposition III.3 directly yields the Rule of Tangents (\(\tan \widehat{AB} : \tan \widehat{\Delta E} = \sin \widehat{A\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{\Delta Z}\)).
