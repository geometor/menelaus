Analytical Summary: Ptolemy’s Chords vs. Menelaus’s Sines¶

Modern Elements-2.0 Comparison of Ptolemaic and Menelaean Formulations

1. Ptolemy’s Chord Formulation¶

In Almagest I.13, Ptolemy expresses the transversal theorem in terms of the chord of double the arc:

\[\frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{\Gamma E})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{EA})} \;=\; \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{\Gamma Z})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{Z\Delta})} \cdot \frac{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{\Delta B})}{\operatorname{crd}(2\widehat{BA})}.\]

Because Ptolemy’s table of chords (\(R = 60^p\)) was calibrated for astronomical interpolation, this formulation directly fed his calculations of: - Declination circles and right ascensions (Syntaxis I.14–16). - Rising times in sphaera recta and sphaera obliqua (Syntaxis II.7–9).

2. Structural Equivalence¶

Since \(\operatorname{crd}(2\theta) = 2R \sin \theta\), the factor \(2R\) cancels in each of the three ratios, making Ptolemy’s chord identity mathematically identical to Menelaus’s sine theorem:

\[\frac{2R \sin \widehat{\Gamma E}}{2R \sin \widehat{EA}} \;=\; \frac{2R \sin \widehat{\Gamma Z}}{2R \sin \widehat{Z\Delta}} \cdot \frac{2R \sin \widehat{\Delta B}}{2R \sin \widehat{BA}} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \frac{\sin \widehat{\Gamma E}}{\sin \widehat{EA}} \;=\; \frac{\sin \widehat{\Gamma Z}}{\sin \widehat{Z\Delta}} \cdot \frac{\sin \widehat{\Delta B}}{\sin \widehat{BA}}.\]
