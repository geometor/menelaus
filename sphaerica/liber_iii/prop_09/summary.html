Analytical Summary: Incenter Concurrency & Incircle Existence on S²¶

Modern Elements-2.0 Decomposition of Proposition III.9

1. Synthetic Analysis of Angle Bisector Concurrency¶

  • Given: Spherical triangle \(\triangle AB\Gamma\) with bisectors \(A\Delta\) and \(\Gamma\Delta\) meeting at \(\Delta\). Arc \(B\Delta E\) connects vertex \(B\) to opposite side \(A\Gamma\).

  • Demonstrate: Arc \(B\Delta E\) bisects angle \(\angle B\).

  • Proof: - Prop. III.6 on \(\triangle ABE\) with bisector \(A\Delta\): \(\sin \widehat{B\Delta} : \sin \widehat{\Delta E} = \sin \widehat{BA} : \sin \widehat{AE}\). - Prop. III.6 on \(\triangle \Gamma BE\) with bisector \(\Gamma\Delta\): \(\sin \widehat{B\Delta} : \sin \widehat{\Delta E} = \sin \widehat{B\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{\Gamma E}\). - Equating the common ratio: \(\sin \widehat{BA} : \sin \widehat{AE} = \sin \widehat{B\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{\Gamma E}\). - Alternating (permutando): \(\sin \widehat{B\Gamma} : \sin \widehat{BA} = \sin \widehat{\Gamma E} : \sin \widehat{AE}\). - Converse of Prop. III.6 forces \(B\Delta\) to bisect \(\angle B\).

2. The Spherical Incircle Corollary¶

  • The common intersection \(\Delta\) is equidistant from all three sides in spherical perpendicular arc measure.

  • Therefore, a spherical small circle with center \(\Delta\) and radius \(r\) touches all three great-circle sides, establishing the existence of the inscribed circle for every spherical triangle.
