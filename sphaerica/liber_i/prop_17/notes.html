Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Absolute Unit of Length¶

Polar Triangle Duality vs. Menelaus’s Direct Demonstration

1. Modern Proof via Polar Duality¶

Modern textbooks prove AAA spherical congruence by passing to the polar triangle \(\triangle A^*B^*C^*\) (polar_triangle()), where the vertices of the polar triangle are the poles of the sides of the original triangle. By the fundamental polar supplementary relations:

\[a^* \;=\; \pi - A, \qquad b^* \;=\; \pi - B, \qquad c^* \;=\; \pi - C.\]

If two spherical triangles have equal angles (\(A = A'\), \(B = B'\), \(C = C'\)), their polar triangles have equal sides (\(a^* = a^{*\prime}\), \(b^* = b^{*\prime}\), \(c^* = c^{*\prime}\)). By SSS congruence (Proposition I.8), \(\triangle A^*B^*C^* \cong \triangle A^{*\prime}B^{*\prime}C^{*\prime}\), whence their angles are equal, forcing the sides of the original triangles to be equal.

2. Elimination of Homothetic Dilations¶

On any Riemannian 2-manifold with constant Gaussian curvature \(K\), the Gauss–Bonnet theorem establishes:

\[E \;=\; A + B + C - \pi \;=\; K \cdot \text{Area}(\triangle ABC).\]

Because \(K = +1\) on the unit sphere, the angular excess \(E\) directly measures area. If two triangles were similar with sides scaled by \(k \neq 1\), their areas would scale by \(k^2\), requiring different excesses \(E \neq E'\), contradicting the assumption that their angles are identical. Thus positive curvature fixes an absolute unit of length (the quadrant / radius) and eliminates non-congruent scale similarity.
