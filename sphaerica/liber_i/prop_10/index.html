Proposition I.10 — Theorem: Semicircle Exterior-Angle Criterion¶

Menelai Alexandrini Sphaericorum Lib. I Prop. X. Theor. (Halley 1758, pp. 9–10)

Enunciation (Halley 1758, pp. 9–10)

In every spherical triangle, if two sides taken together be equal to a semicircle, when the remaining side is produced, the exterior angle shall be equal to the interior and opposite angle upon the produced side. And if the two sides together be less than a semicircle, then the exterior angle shall be greater than the interior and opposite angle upon the produced side. But if the two sides together be greater than a semicircle, the exterior angle shall be less than the interior angle opposite to it.

f. 0026 · p. 10

Let \(\triangle \mathrm{AB\Gamma}\) be a spherical triangle, and let its two sides \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) taken together be equal to a semicircle, and let arc \(\mathrm{A\Gamma}\) be produced: I say that the exterior angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) is equal to the interior angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\) opposite to it. And if the two arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) be less than a semicircle, angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) shall be greater than angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\). But if arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) be greater than a semicircle, angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) shall be less than angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\).

Produce arc \(\mathrm{AB}\) to meet arc \(\mathrm{A\Gamma}\), which let it meet at \(\mathrm{\Delta}\). Now since arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) are equal to a semicircle, and the two arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}\) are also equal to a semicircle, arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}\) will be equal to arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\), and (by Proposition I.2) angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) will be equal to angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Delta\Gamma}\). But angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Delta\Gamma}\) is equal to angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\); wherefore angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) is equal to angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\).

Now let the two arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) together be less than a semicircle; and the two arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}\) together are equal to a semicircle: wherefore arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}\) is greater than arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\), and therefore (by Proposition I.9) angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) is greater than angle \(\angle \mathrm{\Delta}\). But angle \(\angle \mathrm{\Delta}\) is equal to angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\), for the inclination is the same: wherefore the exterior angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) is greater than the interior angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\).

But if the two arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) together be greater than a semicircle: I say that the exterior angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) is less than the interior angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\) opposite to it. For arc \(\mathrm{AB\Delta}\) is a semicircle, and the two arcs \(\widehat{\mathrm{AB}}, \widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) are greater than a semicircle; wherefore arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{B\Gamma}}\) is greater than arc \(\widehat{\mathrm{B\Delta}}\), and consequently angle \(\angle \mathrm{\Delta}\) is greater than angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\). But angle \(\angle \mathrm{\Delta}\) is equal to angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\): therefore angle \(\angle \mathrm{B\Gamma\Delta}\) is less than angle \(\angle \mathrm{A}\). Quod erat demonstrandum.

And the converse of this is manifest: namely, that if in a spherical triangle, one side being produced, the exterior angle be equal to the interior and opposite angle, then the remaining two arcs taken together are equal to a semicircle; and if the exterior angle be greater than the interior and opposite, the arcs together shall be less than a semicircle; and if the exterior angle be less than the interior and opposite, the remaining sides of the triangle taken together shall be greater than a semicircle.
