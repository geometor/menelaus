Scholarly & GEOMETOR Notes: Exact Rational Excess¶

Exact Algebraic Proof via Euler–Eriksson Formula in geometor.model

1. Exact Rational Spherical Excess¶

In spherical_excess_report(), for any three rational vertices \(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C} \in S^2(\mathbb{Q})\) lying in an open hemisphere, the Euler–Eriksson formula expresses the tangent of half the spherical excess \(E = A + B + C - \pi\) as an exact rational number with zero square roots or inverse trigonometric approximations:

\[\tan\!\left(\frac{E}{2}\right) \;=\; \frac{|\det(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C})|}{1 + \mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} + \mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{C} + \mathbf{C}\cdot\mathbf{A}} \;\in\; \mathbb{Q}_{> 0}.\]

2. Canonical Rational Verification¶

For our canonical configuration in geometor.menelaus.spherical:

\[\mathbf{A} = (0, 0, 1), \quad \mathbf{B} = \left(\frac{3}{5}, \frac{4}{5}, 0\right), \quad \mathbf{C} = \left(\frac{2}{3}, \frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{3}\right):\]

  • Determinant volume scalar: \(\det(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{B}, \mathbf{C}) = \frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{3} - \frac{4}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{3} = \frac{6}{15} - \frac{8}{15} = -\frac{2}{15}\).

  • Scalar product sum: \(1 + 0 + \left(\frac{6}{15} + \frac{8}{15}\right) + \frac{1}{3} = 1 + \frac{14}{15} + \frac{5}{15} = \frac{34}{15}\).

  • Exact half-excess tangent:

\[\tan\left(\frac{E}{2}\right) \;=\; \frac{2/15}{34/15} \;=\; \frac{1}{17} \;>\; 0.\]

Since \(\tan(E/2) = 1/17 > 0\), it follows with 100% algebraic certitude that \(E > 0\), rigorously certifying Menelaus’s Proposition I.11 purely within \(\mathbb{Q}\)!
