Symbolic Demonstrations¶

100% Exact SymPy & geometor.model Constructions of Planar, Spherical & Dual Menelaus Geometry

Every construction in geometor.menelaus executes with zero floating-point approximations over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and closed-form quadratic radical extensions, rendering interactive .euclid-graphic SVGs via scripts/build_figures.py. Hover or click any inline :g: geometric element reference below to highlight its exact counterpart inside the figure!

1. Planar Menelaus & Euclidean Parallel Auxiliary (Halley Lemma I / Almagest I.13)¶

Given reference triangle ABC (A = (3, 6), B = (0, 0), C = (9, 0)) and transversal DF cutting extended side BC externally at D ((12, 0)), side CA internally at E ((7, 2)), and side AB internally at F ((2, 4)), drawing auxiliary segment CH through C parallel to DF ([I.31]) meets AB at H ((3/2, 3)):

\[\frac{\overline{AF}}{\overline{FB}} \cdot \frac{\overline{BD}}{\overline{DC}} \cdot \frac{\overline{CE}}{\overline{EA}} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (-4) \cdot \frac{1}{2} = -1, \qquad \left|\frac{AF}{FB} \cdot \frac{BD}{DC} \cdot \frac{CE}{EA}\right| = +1.\]

Constructed symbolically via construct_menelaus_planar().

2. Wildberger Rational Trigonometry Formulation (Quadrance & Spread Laws)¶

Using quadrance() and line_spread() (menelaus_rational_trig_invariants()), Planar Menelaus on ABC and transversal DF holds purely over \(\mathbb{Q}\) with zero square roots:

\[\frac{Q(A, F)\,Q(B, D)\,Q(C, E)}{Q(F, B)\,Q(D, C)\,Q(E, A)} = \frac{s(t, b)}{s(t, c)} \cdot \frac{s(t, c)}{s(t, a)} \cdot \frac{s(t, a)}{s(t, b)} = 1.\]

3. Converse of Menelaus Gallery (Four Projective Collinearity Theorems)¶

3.1 External Angle-Bisector Axis (Anti-Orthic Axis, cf. Sphaerica III.6)¶

On the scalene Heronian triangle ABC (A = (-9, 12), B = (0, 0), C = (7, 0), side lengths a = 7, b = 20, c = 15, integer incenter I = (1, 2)), construct_external_bisector_axis() constructs the three external angle bisectors meeting the opposite sides at De = (-21, 0), Ee = (21, -21/2), and Fe = (63/13, -84/13), whose signed ratio product \((-c/a)(-b/c)(-a/b) = (-15/7)(-20/15)(-7/20) = -1\) places De, Ee, and Fe on the anti-orthic axis DeEe!

3.2 Desargues’s Two-Triangle Perspectivity Axis¶

In construct_desargues_axis(), applying Planar Menelaus three times to perspective ray-triangles \(\triangle OBC\), \(\triangle OCA\), and \(\triangle OAB\) from center O cancels the radial ratios along OA1, OB1, and OC1, proving that corresponding side intersections D, E, and F lie on Desargues’s axis DF.

3.3 Pascal’s Hexagrammum Mysticum¶

In construct_pascal_hexagram(), inscribing six rational vertices P1, P2, P3, P4, P5, P6 on \(x^2 + y^2 = 100\) and forming the diagonal triangle U, V, W of alternating sides, three applications of Planar Menelaus combined with Euclid III.35/III.36 power-of-a-point chord products prove that opposite-side intersections L, M, and N are collinear on Pascal’s Mystic Line LM.

3.4 Monge’s Three-Circle Similicenter Theorem¶

In construct_monge_exsimilicenters(), for circles centered at A, B, C with exact radii \(r_A = 1, r_B = 2, r_C = 4\), the three external centers of similitude Sbc, Sca, and Sab satisfy \((-r_A/r_B)(-r_B/r_C)(-r_C/r_A) = (-1/2)(-2/4)(-4/1) = -1\), placing them on Monge’s external axis SbcSca.

4. Unit-Sphere Intrinsic Geometry, Spherical Menelaus & Cross-Ratio Pencil¶

4.1 Spherical Excess (\(A+B+C > \pi\)) & Polar Triangle AAA Congruence (Sphaerica I.11 & I.17)¶

Via polar_triangle(), spherical triangle A, B, C (AB, BC, CA) and its supplementary polar triangle A, B, C satisfy \(\cos a^* = -\cos A\), confirming that three dihedral angles uniquely determine all three arc lengths on \(S^2\) (Sphaerica Prop. I.17).

5. Projective Point-Line Duality: Ceva (+1) vs. Menelaus (-1) & Trilinear Polars¶

Given perspector P = (3, 3) inside ABC, construct_trilinear_polar() constructs its concurrent cevians meeting the sides at Dc, Ec, Fc (Ceva product = +1). Intersecting the cevian sides with the extended sides of ABC produces the harmonic conjugates Dm, Em, Fm, satisfying \((B, C; D_c, D_m) = (C, A; E_c, E_m) = (A, B; F_c, F_m) = -1\), negating all three ratios \((-1)^3(+1) = -1\) and aligning them on the trilinear polar DmEm!
